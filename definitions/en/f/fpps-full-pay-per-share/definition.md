Method for calculating miner payouts in mining pools. It is an evolution of the PPS (Pay Per Share) method. It pays miners not only for each valid share they submit, but also includes a portion of the transaction fees. The payout is calculated from the average transaction fees of previous blocks and the pool's hashrate. Miners are therefore paid for the shares they submit whether or not a block is found, and this method also pays out the expected value. It gives miners a stable, predictable payout, because it eliminates the variance tied to the probability of finding a block, while still exposing them to fluctuations in the fee market. It is, however, riskier for pool operators, who must pay miners even when no block is found and thereby absorb the variance risk.
