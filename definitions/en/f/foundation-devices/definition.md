American company cofounded in Boston in 2020 by Zach Herbert, Jacob Johnston, Ken Carpenter, and Matt Beach, specializing in open-source hardware for securing bitcoin. Its flagship product, Passport, is a hardware wallet that works air-gapped, through QR codes and microSD cards, with no direct network connection. Foundation Devices also develops Envoy, a companion mobile app that serves as the interface for managing the Passport and interacting with the Bitcoin network. All of the hardware is assembled in the United States, and the firmware is fully open source.
