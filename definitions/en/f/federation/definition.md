Group of participants who collaborate to administer a network or a system, striking a compromise between centralization and decentralization. In a federation, responsibility is spread among several independent entities rather than concentrated in a single one, which eliminates single points of failure and strengthens the resilience of the system.

In the Bitcoin ecosystem, the federated model is used notably by the Liquid network, where a set of functionaries collectively sign blocks and manage the peg-in and peg-out operations between the main chain and the sidechain. The Fedimint protocol also relies on a federated model to manage Chaumian mints. The concept of Strong Federations, formalized by Blockstream, describes a system in which functionaries operate under strict consensus rules, without requiring individual trust in each member.
