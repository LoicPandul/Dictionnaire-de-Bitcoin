Proportional component of routing fees on the Lightning Network. For each forwarded payment, a node can charge a fee rate calculated in proportion to the routed amount, expressed in parts per million (ppm). For example, a fee rate of 100 ppm means that the node charges 100 satoshis for every million satoshis routed. The fee rate complements the base fee, which is a fixed amount per payment. Adjusting the fee rate is an essential lever in managing a routing node: a rate that is too high deters traffic, while one that is too low does not compensate for the cost of locking up liquidity in the channel.
