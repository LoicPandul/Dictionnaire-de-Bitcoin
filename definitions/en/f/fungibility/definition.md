Property of money ensuring that each unit is interchangeable with, and indistinguishable from, any other similar unit. Bitcoin, as a unit of account, is in principle fungible, because one bitcoin is always worth another: 1 BTC = 1 BTC. However, the traceability of UTXOs (the medium that carries the units) on the blockchain can sometimes compromise this fungibility. Each satoshi can be distinguished by its history, which gives it a specific identity. How parties perceive the history of each UTXO can influence their judgment of the fungibility of the bitcoin used. Thus, although fungibility is an intrinsic characteristic of monetary units, it can be altered by the specifics of the medium used to carry those units, as is the case with Bitcoin.
