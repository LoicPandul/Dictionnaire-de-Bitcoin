Attack that consists of sending tiny amounts of bitcoin to a large number of receiving addresses. The attacker's goal is to push the recipients to combine these amounts with other UTXOs. The attacker then tracks the future movements of these small amounts of bitcoin, with the aim of forming clusters of addresses, that is, determining whether several addresses belong to the same entity. By cross-referencing the information gathered during the attack with other data and heuristics used in chain analysis, the attacker can identify certain entities and their associated addresses. This method is a threat only to users' privacy, and does not affect the security of their funds.

*The original term for this attack is “dusting attack,” but some bitcoiners suggest using “forced address reuse” instead, because they find the term “dust” inappropriate here.*
