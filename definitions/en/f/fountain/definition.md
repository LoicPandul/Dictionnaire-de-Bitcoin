Podcast app that integrates Lightning payments following the value-for-value model. Founded in 2021 by Oscar Merry and Nick Malster, Fountain lets listeners send micropayments in satoshis directly to content creators in real time while listening, over the Lightning Network. The app is built on the Podcasting 2.0 standard initiated by Adam Curry, which natively embeds Bitcoin payments in RSS feeds. Listeners can also earn satoshis by listening to podcasts, recommending content, or interacting with the community. Fountain is available on iOS and Android.
