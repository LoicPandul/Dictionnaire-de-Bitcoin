Website or application that gives away small amounts of bitcoin to its visitors for free, usually in exchange for solving a captcha or a simple task. The term "faucet" refers to the idea of dispensing money drop by drop.

The first Bitcoin faucet was created by Gavin Andresen in June 2010 and gave away 5 BTC per visitor. In total, about 19,700 BTC were given away by this site, with the aim of making Bitcoin known and encouraging its adoption at a time when the cryptocurrency had almost no market value.

In 2026, faucets are found mainly on test networks (testnet, signet), where they supply test bitcoin to developers. On mainnet, faucets have essentially disappeared because of bitcoin's high value and the proliferation of bots.
