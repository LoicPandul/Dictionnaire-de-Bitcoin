Bitcoin mining pool based in the United States, operated by Foundry Digital, a subsidiary of Digital Currency Group (DCG). Launched in 2020, Foundry USA Pool has become one of the largest mining pools in the world by hashrate, regularly ranking first. It provides mining services to institutional operators and large North American mining farms. Foundry also offers financing, hardware procurement, and advisory services to professional miners. Foundry USA's large share of Bitcoin's hashrate raises questions about the geographic centralization of mining, since most of the pool's computing power is concentrated in the United States.
