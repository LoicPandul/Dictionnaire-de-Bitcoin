Protocol for paying with and managing bitcoin, designed to improve privacy and reduce reliance on the main chain by pooling the custody of funds. Fedimint was created by Eric Sirion in 2021. It relies on a Chaumian banking system which, instead of being centralized around a single trusted party, relies on federations. These federations are groups of trusted guardians who collectively hold and manage the bitcoin of the users in their group. Within the group, users can make payments with notes issued in exchange for their bitcoin deposit. The idea of Fedimint is to deploy this concept at the level of local communities. It is therefore a kind of evolution of the deposit bank based on bitcoin, with David Chaum's eCash system and the use of a federation of trusted people in charge of holding the deposit and issuing the underlying asset.
