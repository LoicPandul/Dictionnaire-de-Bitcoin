Open-source mobile app for buying and selling bitcoin peer-to-peer, with no identity verification (KYC). Launched in 2022, Vexl is operated by the Vexl Foundation, a nonprofit supported by SatoshiLabs, the company behind Trezor.

The app relies on an original principle: counterparties are discovered through the user's social graph, that is, their phone contacts and the contacts of their contacts. This mechanism reproduces digitally the trust of in-person trades.

All communications are end-to-end encrypted, no personal data is stored, and each public profile is unique to each transaction.
