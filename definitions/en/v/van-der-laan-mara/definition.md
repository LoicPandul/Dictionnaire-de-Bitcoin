Lead maintainer of Bitcoin Core from 2014 to 2022 (the merge key was removed in February 2023), known by the GitHub handle laanwj. Earlier signing name: Wladimir J. van der Laan. Since December 2025, the GitHub profile and the last commit in Bitcoin Core (December 7, 2025) bear the name "Mara van der Laan".

On May 7, 2011, Van der Laan wrote the first commit of the Bitcoin-Qt graphical interface, then merged contributions into Bitcoin Core until July 2022.

On April 7, 2014, Gavin Andresen announced that he was entrusting Van der Laan with the role of "Bitcoin Core Maintainer". Van der Laan had by then been working full time on Bitcoin Core for several months, thanks to donations from Bitcoin Foundation members. The lead maintainer coordinated the project (releases, signing, meetings) in addition to merging PRs, like the other maintainers. On January 21, 2021, Van der Laan announced plans to delegate these tasks. On February 7, 2023, Van der Laan opened the PR that removes Van der Laan's own key from the repository's list of trusted keys; it was merged on February 16, 2023. No successor has been named: Bitcoin Core no longer has a lead maintainer.

Van der Laan is the author of BIP-0155 (`addrv2`), proposed on February 27, 2019, which extends the peer address format to accommodate Tor v3, I2P, and other networks.
