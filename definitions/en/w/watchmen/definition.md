In Liquid (a Bitcoin sidechain), entities in charge of maintaining the peg of L-BTC, Liquid's native token, by managing and securing the underlying BTC. They make sure that assets transferred between the main Bitcoin blockchain and the Liquid sidechain are properly locked and unlocked. The aim of their actions is to maintain the same value between the L-BTC circulating on Liquid and the BTC circulating on Bitcoin. In Liquid, watchmen are among the functionaries, along with the blocksigners.
