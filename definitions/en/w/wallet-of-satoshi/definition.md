Mobile wallet specialized in payments over the Lightning Network. Wallet of Satoshi is designed to offer a simplified user experience: users can send and receive Lightning payments in seconds, with no technical configuration.

Historically, Wallet of Satoshi ran on a custodial model: the company held the private keys and managed the funds on the user's behalf. This model simplifies use but involves a tradeoff: users must trust the provider to safeguard their bitcoin. Since 2025, WoS also offers an optional integration of the Spark protocol, making a self-custodial model possible.
