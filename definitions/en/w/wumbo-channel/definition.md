Large-capacity Lightning channel exceeding the historical limit of 16,777,215 satoshis (about 0.168 BTC) imposed by default on the first nodes. This limit, encoded on 24 bits, was introduced as a precaution during the first years of the Lightning Network to limit potential losses in case of bugs in the implementations.

Subsequently, implementations introduced an option to accept channels of up to 10 BTC. These high-capacity channels are nicknamed “wumbo channels,” in reference to an episode of *SpongeBob SquarePants*.
