Bitcoin Core developer, author of several major BIPs. He has contributed code to Bitcoin Core since 2011 and was one of its maintainers: his merge key was removed from the list of trusted keys by PR 25568, merged on July 8, 2022 (commit of July 7).

He wrote BIP-0032, which defines hierarchical deterministic (HD) wallets, dated February 11, 2012. He also wrote BIP-0066, on strict DER signatures (2015), then coauthored SegWit with Eric Lombrozo and Johnson Lau (BIP-0141, dated December 21, 2015, and BIP-0143). He is a coauthor of BIP-0173, the Bech32 address format (2017), with Greg Maxwell, and of BIP-0324, the version 2 encrypted transport (draft of 2019, published as a BIP in 2023).

In 2014, he was one of the cofounders of Blockstream, then joined Chaincode Labs as an engineer. On January 19, 2020, three BIP numbers were assigned to him for Taproot: BIP-0340 (Schnorr signatures), BIP-0341 (spending rules), and BIP-0342 (script validation). He is also a coauthor of BIP-0379, which specifies Miniscript.

Starting in 2024, he wrote the code for cluster linearization and TxGraph, two building blocks of the cluster mempool shipped with Bitcoin Core 31.0, which replaces the old ancestor and descendant limits with cluster limits. On GitHub, he goes by sipa.
