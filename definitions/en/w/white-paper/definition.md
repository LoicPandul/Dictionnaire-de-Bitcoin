Report or guide that concisely informs readers about a complex issue. In the case of Bitcoin, the name given to the scientific document titled *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*, first published by Satoshi Nakamoto on October 31, 2008. This document describes the fundamental principles of Bitcoin. The white paper presented today is a second version, published on March 24, 2009.

*To download the Bitcoin white paper: https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf.*
