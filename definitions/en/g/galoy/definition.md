Company founded in 2019 by Nicolas Burtey and Chris Hunter, developing open-source Bitcoin banking infrastructure. Galoy is behind the Blink wallet (formerly “Bitcoin Beach Wallet”), the app that underpinned the circular Bitcoin economy of El Zonte, El Salvador, before the country adopted bitcoin as legal tender in 2021. The Galoy platform lets organizations deploy their own Bitcoin and Lightning banking infrastructure, either self-hosted or as a managed service (Banking-as-a-Service). The company also develops bitcoin-backed lending solutions.
