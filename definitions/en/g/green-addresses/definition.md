Old Bitcoin address proposal whose outgoing transactions are pre-approved by a trusted third party. This concept lets parties receiving funds through such an address treat them as immediately reliable, without waiting for them to be included in a block. It rests on trust in the service that controls the green address, because it guarantees that the funds sent have not been and will not be double spent. This type of service was once used to speed up transactions (zero-conf), but it is no longer used today.
