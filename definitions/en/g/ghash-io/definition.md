Former Bitcoin mining pool operated by CEX.IO, a company based in the United Kingdom. Ghash.io is best known for briefly exceeding 51% of the Bitcoin network's total hashrate in June 2014, which raised concrete fears of a 51% attack. Although the pool did not exploit this dominant position for malicious purposes, the event served as a wake-up call for the Bitcoin community and illustrated the centralization risks of mining pools. Many miners voluntarily left Ghash.io in the following days to redistribute hashrate to other pools. Ghash.io then gradually lost influence before ceasing operations.
