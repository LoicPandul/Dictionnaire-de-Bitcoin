Method of calculating miners' pay in the context of mining pools. This payment system is based on a score and designed to counter pool hopping. It ensures that the payment per submitted share stays constant, regardless of when it is submitted. Miners accumulate scores, calculated with a decay factor, and payments are calculated at the end of the round. They are proportional to the miners' scores. This method involves variable and fixed fees for the miner and reduces the variance of payments per share.
