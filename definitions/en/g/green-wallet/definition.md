Bitcoin wallet software available on PC, Android, and iOS, developed by Blockstream since it acquired the GreenAddress software in 2016. It includes several features, such as multisig protection with two-factor authentication, and it is compatible with most hardware wallets. It is simple to pick up and can be used by beginners. Since May 2025, Blockstream has offered a redesigned mobile app, the Blockstream App, built on the foundation of Green.
