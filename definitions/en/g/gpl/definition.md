Acronym for *GNU General Public License*. Series of free software licenses originally created by the Free Software Foundation. It guarantees users the freedom to use, modify, and distribute the software. The GPL does require, however, that any derived or modified software also be distributed under the same license, which ensures that the original freedoms are preserved in every version of the software. The GPL is often used for open source projects and is sometimes found on Bitcoin-related projects. The original license of Satoshi Nakamoto's Bitcoin client, however, was an MIT X11 license, and that is still the case for Bitcoin Core.
