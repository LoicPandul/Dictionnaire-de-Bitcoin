Pejorative term frequently used in the cryptocurrency world to describe altcoins considered worthless or without a future. The term is used in two distinct contexts: in the general cryptocurrency community, it refers to a project that lacks technological innovation, has no concrete use case, or is mainly the product of excessive speculation. For maximalist bitcoiners, the term covers all cryptocurrencies other than bitcoin (BTC), making “shitcoin” a synonym for “altcoin”. The word is a blend of “shit” and “coin”. The term was first used in a post by user Ribuck on November 8, 2010, on the Bitcoin Talk forum.
