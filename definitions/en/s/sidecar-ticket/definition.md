Token that allows the beneficiary of a sidecar channel bought on Lightning Pool to claim the opening of the channel. When a participant in a Pool auction buys a channel for a third party (for example a mobile wallet), the participant generates a sidecar ticket containing the information needed for the opening. The beneficiary presents this ticket so that the seller opens the channel directly to them. This mechanism allows users who do not take part in Pool auctions directly to benefit from inbound liquidity.
