Stockpile of bitcoin held by a state, an institution, or a company as protection against economic instability, currency debasement, or geopolitical risks.

On March 6, 2025, a US presidential executive order created a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve. It is funded by the bitcoin that the Department of the Treasury holds following final forfeitures, and the bitcoin deposited in it must not be sold. The order provides that any additional acquisition be made at no additional cost to taxpayers.
