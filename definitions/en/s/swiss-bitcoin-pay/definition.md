Bitcoin point-of-sale solution for merchants and businesses. Swiss Bitcoin Pay lets merchants accept bitcoin payments, both on-chain and over the Lightning Network, through a mobile app or an API that can be integrated into existing checkout systems.

The service runs on a hybrid model: it handles payment reception without the merchant having to run a node or any technical infrastructure, then pays out the takings automatically every day. The funds received can be kept in bitcoin, automatically converted to fiat currency, or split between the two, depending on the merchant's preferences.

Swiss Bitcoin Pay sits between fully self-hosted solutions such as BTCPay Server and custodial payment processors, offering a compromise between ease of use and sovereignty over funds.
