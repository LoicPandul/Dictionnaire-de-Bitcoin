Type of sighash flag used in Bitcoin transaction signatures to indicate that the signature applies to all inputs of the transaction and to a single output, the one matching the index of the signed input. Each input signed with `SIGHASH_SINGLE` is thus bound to one particular output. The other outputs are not committed to by the signature and can therefore be modified later. This type of signature is useful in complex transactions, where participants want to bind certain inputs to specific outputs while leaving flexibility for the other outputs of the transaction.
