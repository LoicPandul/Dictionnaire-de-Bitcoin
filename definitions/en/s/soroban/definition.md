Encrypted communication protocol built on Tor that allows collaboration with other users in a Cahoots transaction. Soroban was developed by the Samourai Wallet team to facilitate the exchange of partially signed bitcoin transactions (PSBTs) between users who want to carry out collaborative transactions (Stowaway, Stonewall x2...). The protocol was used by the Samourai Wallet app, which ceased operations in April 2024, and was integrated into Sparrow Wallet, which removed it in version 1.9.0 (April 2024).
