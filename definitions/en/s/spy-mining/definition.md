Practice in which a miner or mining pool connects to a competing pool in order to receive the block header hash as soon as that pool discovers a new block. By obtaining this information before it is propagated across the network, the spying miner can immediately start mining on top of the new block, without waiting for the network to receive and fully validate it.

This technique gives an advantage of a few seconds over miners who wait for the block to propagate normally. Spy mining is a common practice in the mining industry, and it contributes to a competitive environment in which pools compete not only with the rest of the network but also with one another. It is often associated with validationless mining, because the spying miner starts working on a new block without having verified all of its transactions.
