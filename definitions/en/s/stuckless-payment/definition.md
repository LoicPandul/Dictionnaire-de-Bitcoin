Proposal to solve the problem of stuck payments on the Lightning Network. With current HTLCs, a payment can remain stuck, neither completed nor canceled, for example if an intermediate node goes offline or deliberately holds the HTLC. The sender then has to wait for the timelock to expire to recover their funds, and cannot retry the same payment without risking paying the recipient twice.

Stuckless payments, proposed by Hiroki Gondo, rely on PTLCs and add an extra round of communication between the sender and the recipient when the payment is set up and settled. This mechanism introduces an acknowledgment key that the sender controls: the recipient can finalize the payment only after receiving this key. The sender can thus launch several attempts in parallel on different routes, with the guarantee that if one succeeds, all the others will automatically fail. This eliminates the risk of a double payment and improves routing reliability on Lightning.
