Privacy technique that lets a recipient receive payments on single-use addresses, derived on the fly by the sender, without those addresses being publicly linkable to the recipient's identity. The principle rests on elliptic curve cryptography: the recipient publishes a static public key (the stealth address), and each sender generates an ephemeral receiving address by combining this public key with a shared secret derived through ECDH (Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman). Only the recipient, thanks to their private key, can identify and spend the funds received on these ephemeral addresses. This approach avoids address reuse. The concept of the stealth address was first proposed by a BitcoinTalk forum user under the pseudonym "ByteCoin" in 2011, then formalized by Peter Todd in January 2014, and served as the foundation for the creation of BIP-0047 and Silent Payments.
