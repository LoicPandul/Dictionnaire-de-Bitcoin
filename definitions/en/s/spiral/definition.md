Subsidiary of Block Inc. (formerly Square) dedicated to funding and developing open-source Bitcoin projects. Created in 2019 as “Square Crypto”, then renamed “Spiral” in 2021, the organization funds independent developers, designers, and contributors working on Bitcoin and its ecosystem.

Spiral has notably supported projects such as Bitcoin Core and BTCPay Server. The organization also develops the Lightning Dev Kit (LDK) and the Bitcoin Dev Kit (BDK), two open-source libraries that make it easier to integrate Bitcoin and Lightning into third-party applications.
