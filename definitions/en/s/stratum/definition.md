Network protocol layered on top of Bitcoin, designed specifically to optimize communication between individual miners and mining pool servers in order to increase their profitability. Stratum was announced in late 2012 by Marek Palatinus, better known by the pseudonym "Slush" and founder of the very first mining pool, Slush Pool, now renamed Braiins Pool. Stratum replaced the older Getwork protocol, which had become obsolete.

It is important to understand that Stratum is not built into Bitcoin as a rule, but is rather specific software used by pools. Although mining pools are free not to use it, Stratum has become the standard for mining on Bitcoin for more than ten years. Its second version, Stratum V2, aims to improve on Stratum and to reduce the centralization concerns around mining pools that the original protocol has fueled.
