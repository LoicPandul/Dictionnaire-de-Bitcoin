Practice by which a user keeps direct control of their private keys, and therefore of their bitcoin, without depending on an external entity to manage their assets. It contrasts with custody by a platform (exchange, online service), which holds the keys in place of the user. The user keeps the keys in a wallet, such as a hardware wallet, and backs up their mnemonic phrase. In return, the user alone bears responsibility for that backup: if the mnemonic phrase is lost, no one can restore access to the bitcoin.
