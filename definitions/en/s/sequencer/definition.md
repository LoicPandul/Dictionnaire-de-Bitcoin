Specialized node responsible for ordering transactions and producing blocks in a rollup. The sequencer continuously monitors the mempool, selects pending transactions (prioritized by fees), executes them against the current state of the rollup, and produces a new block at regular intervals. It then issues a signed "soft confirmation", providing a fast pre-confirmation to the full nodes of the network. Periodically, the sequencer publishes commitments to the base layer (Bitcoin, in the case of Citrea) to permanently anchor the order of the blocks. The sequencer can influence the order of transactions and cause liveness delays, but cannot falsify the state of the rollup or steal funds, because validity proofs and the availability of on-chain data guarantee the integrity of state transitions. Sequencer decentralization is an active research topic in the rollup ecosystem.
