Acronym for *Secure Hash Algorithm 512*. It is a cryptographic hash function producing a 512-bit hash digest. It was designed by the National Security Agency (NSA) in the early 2000s. For Bitcoin, the `SHA512` function is not used directly by the protocol, but it is used at the application level, in wallets: BIP-0039 derives the seed from the mnemonic phrase with `PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512`, and BIP-0032 derives the master key and then the child keys with `HMAC-SHA512`. In these processes, it is used several times in the `HMAC` algorithm, as well as in the `PBKDF2` key derivation function. The `SHA512` function belongs to the SHA-2 family, like `SHA256`. Its operation is in fact very similar to that of the latter.
