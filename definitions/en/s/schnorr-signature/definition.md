Digital signature algorithm based on elliptic curve cryptography (ECC). It is used on Bitcoin to sign a transaction with a private key and to verify that signature with the corresponding public key. On Bitcoin, like ECDSA, Schnorr relies on the `secp256k1` elliptic curve, characterized by the equation $y^2 = x^3 + 7$. The Schnorr signature protocol has been implemented in the Bitcoin protocol since November 2021, with the activation of the Taproot upgrade.
