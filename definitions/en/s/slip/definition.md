Acronym for *SatoshiLabs Improvement Proposals*. A set of proposals aimed at improving or standardizing the use of Bitcoin, issued by SatoshiLabs, the company behind the Trezor hardware wallets. These proposals are often structured as extensions of BIPs (Bitcoin Improvement Proposals), with the aim of enriching existing standards. They set out technical decisions taken by SatoshiLabs that do not fit in the BIPs but remain relevant to other wallet software or hardware wallet developers, notably to help standardize processes.
