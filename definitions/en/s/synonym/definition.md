Technology company founded by John Carvalho, focused on developing software and protocols that support a peer-to-peer economy centered on Bitcoin. Synonym is behind the Slashtags protocol, a decentralized web standard that derives additional key pairs from a BIP-0039 seed to create profiles, contacts, and passwordless authentication. The company also developed Bitkit, a mobile wallet with an embedded Lightning node. The repository of the JavaScript implementation of Slashtags is archived on GitHub, with a last push in August 2023, and as of October 2026 the Synonym website presents Pubky, an open protocol for self-hosted identities and data, alongside Bitkit.
