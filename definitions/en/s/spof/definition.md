Acronym for *Single Point of Failure*. In computing, a component or element of a system whose failure would bring the entire system to a halt or cause a significant loss of functionality across it. It can be a piece of hardware, a piece of information, a piece of software, or a part of a network. For example, in the specific context of Bitcoin HD wallets, the 12- or 24-word mnemonic phrase is often a SPOF for the wallet. If its secrecy is not ensured, the entire wallet could be stolen. In the same way, its mere loss could result in the loss of all the bitcoin in the wallet.
