Dark web platform founded by Ross Ulbricht, who went by the pseudonym Dread Pirate Roberts. Launched in 2011, this online marketplace let users buy and sell lawful and unlawful goods and services while preserving their anonymity through Tor and bitcoin payments. Silk Road offered a wide range of items, mostly drugs and medicines that normally require a prescription, but banned anything deemed contrary to natural-law principles, such as hitman services or child sexual abuse material.

The platform reflected the agorist convictions of Ross Ulbricht, promoting a free, unregulated market beyond the reach of government control and taxation. Silk Road played a significant role in Bitcoin's adoption in the early 2010s, helping to raise its profile and spread its use beyond cypherpunk circles.

Silk Road was shut down in 2013. Ross Ulbricht was arrested the same year and sentenced to two life terms plus 40 years without the possibility of parole, along with $183 million in restitution. The sentence raises questions about the proportionality of penalties for nonviolent online crimes. Ross Ulbricht was finally pardoned by President Donald Trump on January 21, 2025, after spending more than 11 years in prison.
