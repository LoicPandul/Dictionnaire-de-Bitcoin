Group of operators responsible for managing shared keys and signing operations within the Spark protocol. The SE collectively holds one half of the spending key associated with the UTXOs locked in Spark, the other half being held by the user. During a transfer on Spark, the SE takes part in refreshing the key shares: it destroys its share associated with the previous owner and generates a new one with the recipient, which makes the old share cryptographically unusable.

The SE is not a single entity but a set of independent Spark operators (SOs) that cooperate through a threshold signature protocol based on Schnorr signatures. No individual operator holds the full key. The security model rests on a 1-of-n assumption: it takes only one honest SO to prevent any collusion with a former owner. Trust is required only at the moment of the transfer: once the old keys are destroyed, operators can no longer affect the funds, even if they are compromised later.
