Method of calculating miners' payouts in the context of mining pools. This reward system is proportional, but weighted by the time at which the share is submitted. The SCORE method values shares according to the time elapsed since the start of the mining cycle. The later in the cycle a share is submitted, the higher its value. This method gives miners an incentive to stay, because each time a miner stops mining, their score stalls while the scores of the others rise ever faster.

*This method is sometimes also called “Bitcoin Pooled Mining” (BPM).*
