American company (Start9 Labs, Inc.) developing StartOS, a free and open-source operating system for personal servers. Like Umbrel, Start9 aims to democratize self-hosting by letting non-technical users install and manage open-source applications on their own hardware, without relying on third-party cloud services.

Start9 sells preconfigured servers and offers a marketplace of self-hosted applications, including Bitcoin Core, Lightning implementations, storage and communication solutions, and more.
