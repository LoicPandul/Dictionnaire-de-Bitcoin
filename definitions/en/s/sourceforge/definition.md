Web platform for hosting and distributing open source software, founded in 1999. SourceForge played a historic role in the early days of Bitcoin: the project was registered there by Satoshi Nakamoto in November 2008, and the first version of the software (v0.1) was released there in January 2009. The platform also hosted the original discussion forum of the Bitcoin community, as well as the bitcoin-dev mailing list in its early days. The Bitcoin project later migrated to GitHub, which became the main collaboration tool for protocol development.
