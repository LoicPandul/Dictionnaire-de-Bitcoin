Bitcoin gambling site launched in April 2012 by Erik Voorhees. The service worked as a probabilistic dice game: players sent bitcoin to addresses corresponding to different winning probabilities and automatically received a payment back depending on the result. The site quickly came to dominate transaction activity on the Bitcoin blockchain. At its peak in 2012 and early 2013, it accounted for nearly half of the transactions recorded on the network.

In July 2013, Erik Voorhees sold the service for 126,315 BTC (about $12.4 million at the time). In June 2014, the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) brought charges against Voorhees for the sale of unregistered securities, related to offerings of SatoshiDice and FeedZeBirds shares to investors in 2012 and 2013. SatoshiDice is considered one of the first large-scale commercial services on Bitcoin.
