Concept originally developed by Andrew Poelstra that allows smart contracts to be executed without explicitly exposing the contract logic on the Bitcoin blockchain. As the name "scriptless scripts" suggests, the idea is to execute scripts (or contracts) without explicitly using scripts. These contracts exploit properties of Schnorr signatures that enable the use of adaptor signatures, notably to perform atomic swaps. The contract's conditions are enforced and executed off-chain by the parties involved, who are the only ones to know its terms. Unlike classic smart contracts, scriptless scripts minimize their footprint on the blockchain, which reduces the cost of the operation. These contracts are also more discreet than classic smart contracts, which leave traces on the blockchain. They therefore look like ordinary transactions, which increases their anonset.
