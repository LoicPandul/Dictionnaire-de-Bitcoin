Stock-market and currency ticker symbol used to represent bitcoin, modeled on the ISO 4217 standard, which governs currency codes. Unlike "BTC", the original symbol, "XBT" follows that standard's convention that the codes of currencies not backed by a state begin with an "X" (like "XAU" for gold). It does not appear in the official ISO 4217 list, but some organizations use it to represent units of bitcoin.
