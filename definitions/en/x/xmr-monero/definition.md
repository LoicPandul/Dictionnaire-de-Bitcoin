Alternative cryptocurrency (altcoin) launched in April 2014, focused on transaction privacy. Monero (XMR) uses several cryptographic techniques to conceal by default the identity of senders, the identity of recipients, and the amounts exchanged: ring signatures, stealth addresses, and the RingCT (Ring Confidential Transactions) protocol. Unlike Bitcoin, where transactions are pseudonymous and traceable on a public blockchain, Monero makes chain analysis considerably harder. Its mining algorithm, RandomX, is designed to resist ASICs and to favor CPU mining.
