Node on the Bitcoin network that initiates a connection to another node, without any involvement from the latter. Up to Bitcoin Core 31, the software allows at most 114 inbound peers by default (out of a total of 125 connections). Since Bitcoin Core 32.0, the default limit is 200 connections, 189 of them inbound, to ease connectivity within the network. Inbound peers are treated with caution, because it cannot be certain that they are honest, given that third parties initiate them. Inbound and outbound peers share the same type of information. The main difference between inbound and outbound peers lies not in the type of information exchanged, but in how these connections are established.
