Anonymous communication network designed to ensure the privacy of exchanges on the Internet. Like Tor, I2P encrypts network data and uses a routing system called garlic routing. This method guarantees the anonymity of communications by passing them through intermediate servers that redirect the information without knowing the identity of either the sender or the recipient. Just like Tor, I2P can be used on a Bitcoin node to secure its network communications.

*The acronym “I2P” stands for “Invisible Internet Project”.*
