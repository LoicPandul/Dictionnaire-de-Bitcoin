Subunit of the satoshi equal to one thousandth of a satoshi, or one hundred-billionth of a bitcoin ($10^{-11}$ BTC). The millisatoshi does not exist on the Bitcoin blockchain, where the satoshi is the smallest indivisible unit. The Lightning Network, on the other hand, uses the millisatoshi as its internal unit of account for payments and for computing routing fees, which allows a finer granularity.
