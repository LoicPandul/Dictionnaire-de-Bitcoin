Any natural or legal person who agrees to exchange a good or a service for bitcoin. It is merchants who give the bitcoin currency its usefulness. The more widely a currency is accepted by merchants, the more useful it becomes to individuals. Since merchants can determine the usefulness of a currency by agreeing to exchange it for goods and services, in the case of Bitcoin they also carry considerable weight in the choice of the consensus rules. Each has a certain power proportional to the economic activity they can bring to a fork. Merchants include, of course, businesses, but also exchanges, miners, and users.
