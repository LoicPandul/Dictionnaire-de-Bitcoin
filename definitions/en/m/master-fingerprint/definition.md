4-byte (32-bit) fingerprint of the master key in a hierarchical deterministic (HD) wallet. It corresponds to the first 4 bytes of the master key identifier (BIP-0032), obtained by computing the `SHA256` hash of the serialized master public key, followed by a `RIPEMD160` hash, a procedure called `HASH160` on Bitcoin. The master fingerprint identifies an HD wallet, independently of derivation paths, but taking into account whether or not a passphrase is used. It is concise information that makes it possible to refer to the origin of a set of keys without revealing sensitive information about the wallet.
