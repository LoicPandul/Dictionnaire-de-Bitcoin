Service for sending and receiving bitcoin by phone, without an internet connection, using the USSD protocol. Created in 2022 by Kgothatso Ngako, a South African developer, the service makes Bitcoin accessible to users of basic phones in Africa. To make a transaction, the user dials a USSD code specific to their country (for example `*920*8333#` in Ghana) and interacts with a text menu. Payments go over the Lightning Network, and Lightning addresses take the form `number@8333.mobi`. Machankura is available in several African countries, including Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa.
