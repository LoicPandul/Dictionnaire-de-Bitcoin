Hash digest, or “top hash,” of a Merkle tree, which represents a summary of all the information in the tree. A Merkle tree is a cryptographic accumulator structure, sometimes also called a “hash tree.” In Bitcoin, Merkle trees are used to organize the transactions in a block and to make it quick to verify that a specific transaction is included. Thus, in Bitcoin blocks, the Merkle root is obtained by successively hashing the transactions in pairs until only a single hash remains (the Merkle root). The latter is then included in the header of the corresponding block. This hash tree is also found in UTREEXO, a structure that condenses nodes' UTXO set, and in Taproot's MAST.
