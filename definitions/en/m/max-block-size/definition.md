`MAX_BLOCK_SIZE` constant that specified the maximum size a block can have on Bitcoin. Historically, this limit was set at 1 MB, a measure put in place by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2010 to prevent spam and maintain a degree of network decentralization. Since SegWit, this constant no longer exists in Bitcoin Core: the limit is enforced through `MAX_BLOCK_WEIGHT`, set at 4,000,000 weight units.
