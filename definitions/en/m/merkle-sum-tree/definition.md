Variant of a Merkle tree in which each node carries not only the hash of the nodes or leaves below it, but also the sum of their values. The root of the tree thus contains the hash of all the data and the total sum of all the leaf values. This structure makes it possible to verify the integrity of the data and the consistency of the amounts at the same time. In the Taproot Assets protocol, Merkle sum trees are used to commit asset balances to the Bitcoin blockchain, ensuring that no asset can be created or destroyed without changing the root of the tree.
