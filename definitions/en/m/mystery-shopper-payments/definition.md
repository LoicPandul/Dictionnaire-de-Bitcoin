Attack in which an attacker sends a small, legitimate bitcoin payment to the target in order to gather information and try to compromise the target's privacy. This technique is often used against merchants. Take the example of Alice, who owns a bakery that accepts bitcoin payments. Eve, the attacker, could buy a croissant to obtain a receiving address of Alice's. Eve could then follow the movements of that UTXO to try to deduce additional information about Alice using tracing heuristics.
