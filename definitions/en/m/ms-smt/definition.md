Acronym for *Merkle Sum Sparse Merkle Tree*. Data structure combining the properties of a Sparse Merkle Tree (SMT) and a Merkle Sum Tree. It makes it possible both to produce proofs of inclusion and non-inclusion (thanks to its "sparse" nature, where each leaf has a position determined by the hash of its key) and to verify the conservation of sums (thanks to the propagation of values through each node up to the root). In the Taproot Assets protocol, the MS-SMT is used to commit assets to the Bitcoin blockchain: its root is embedded in a tapscript of a Taproot transaction. This structure guarantees that it is possible to prove that an asset exists, that an asset does not exist, and that the total of issued assets is consistent, preventing any unauthorized inflation.
