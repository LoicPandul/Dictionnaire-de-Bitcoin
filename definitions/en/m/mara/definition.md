American Bitcoin mining company listed on the NASDAQ under the symbol MARA. Founded in 2010 and initially active in other sectors, it pivoted to Bitcoin mining starting in 2017, under the name Marathon Patent Group. In 2021, it was renamed Marathon Digital Holdings, then MARA Holdings in 2024. The company operates data centers in several US states and ranks among the largest mining companies in the world by hashrate. MARA also operates its own mining pool, MARAPool.
