Finnish programmer, collaborator of Satoshi Nakamoto from 2009, known under the pseudonym Sirius (identifier sirius-m on SourceForge). He presented Bitcoin on the anti-statist forum anti-state.com, which earned him a reply from Satoshi on May 2, 2009: Satoshi asked him to create the project's site on SourceForge and gave him access on May 4. Malmi wrote the FAQ and set up the site bitcoin.sourceforge.net, then built a forum for the project.

In October 2009, he contributed to the Bitcoin code: a Windows installer (commits of October 3 and 4), then a Linux port (commit of October 31).

On October 12, 2009, he sold 5,050 bitcoin for 5.02 dollars to the operator of New Liberty Standard, who was looking to launch an exchange service. In 2014, he presented this sale as the first known trade of bitcoin for dollars, which sets a price of about 0.001 dollars per bitcoin.

In February 2024, he testified by video link from Finland at the COPA trial against Craig Wright before the High Court in London, and published his correspondence with Satoshi on that occasion. On May 20, 2024, the High Court released the reasons for its judgment: Wright is not Satoshi Nakamoto, as the judge had stated orally at the end of the trial, in March 2024.
