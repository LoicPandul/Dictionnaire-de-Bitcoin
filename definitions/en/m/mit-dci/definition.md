MIT Media Lab research program dedicated to digital currencies and distributed ledger technologies. Founded in April 2015, this initiative was the first university group devoted exclusively to Bitcoin. It was created at a time when the Bitcoin Foundation, until then the main source of funding for development, was facing financial difficulties. The DCI funds developers and researchers who work on Bitcoin Core and on the security of the protocol. It thus joins other organizations such as Chaincode Labs, Blockstream, and Spiral in supporting open-source Bitcoin development.
