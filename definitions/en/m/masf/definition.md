Acronym for *Miner-Activated Soft Fork*. Describes a soft fork in Bitcoin whose activation comes from an action by miners. MASFs are a family of soft fork activation mechanisms on Bitcoin. In these approaches, miners signal their agreement and readiness for a protocol upgrade by mining blocks that support locking in the soft fork. If a significant majority of miners votes in favor of the soft fork, the upgrade is considered accepted and is activated later. This process helps avoid splitting the blockchain and keeps the network united. The MASF is preferred for its gentler, more consensual approach, which reduces the risk of a chain split while ensuring that the majority of the computing power supports the new upgrade. The activation mechanisms BIP-0034, BIP-0009, BIP-0008 (if `LOT=false` or if the voting threshold is reached), and Speedy Trial are all MASFs.
