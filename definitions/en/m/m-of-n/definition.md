Threshold multisignature wallet or script. To strengthen the security of bitcoin, a threshold multisignature scheme can be used that requires `m` out of `n` signatures to spend the funds. In an m-of-n, the letter `m` is the required signature threshold and the letter `n` is the total number of existing keys that can sign. For example, in a 2-of-3 setup, two of the three possible signatures are needed to authorize a transaction.
