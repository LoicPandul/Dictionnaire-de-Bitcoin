Peer-to-peer bitcoin exchange protocol without KYC, built on Nostr. Mostro lets users buy and sell bitcoin for fiat currencies over the Lightning Network. The protocol uses Lightning hold invoices as an escrow mechanism: the seller's bitcoin is locked in a pending invoice and released to the buyer only after receipt of the fiat payment is confirmed. Using Nostr as the communication layer makes the system censorship-resistant, unlike centralized solutions. Mostro is written in Rust and is open source.
