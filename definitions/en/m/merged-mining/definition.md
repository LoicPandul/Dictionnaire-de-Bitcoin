Sidechain consensus technique that lets Bitcoin miners work simultaneously on the main chain and on one or more sidechains, without having to perform more computing work. It reuses Bitcoin's proof of work for third-party applications. However, merged mining has a notable drawback for the miner: it requires installing and running node software specific to each sidechain in order to reuse the miner's proofs of work. Moreover, the reward for mining a sidechain is paid on that sidechain, not directly in BTC on the main blockchain.
