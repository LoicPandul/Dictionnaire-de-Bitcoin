Bitcoin Core developer, cofounder of Blockstream, known for having popularized CoinJoin and proposed Taproot. He writes under the name Gregory Maxwell (Greg Maxwell in BIP headers), and under the pseudonym gmaxwell on Bitcointalk.

On August 22, 2013, he published on Bitcointalk “CoinJoin: Bitcoin privacy for the real world”: since each input of a transaction is signed independently, several users can build a single transaction together to blur the link between inputs and outputs. In 2014, he cofounded Blockstream, where he was chief technology officer until January 2018. There he worked on sidechains and confidential transactions. He is a coauthor of BIP-0009 (version bits, dated October 2015) and then of BIP-0173 (Bech32 addresses, written in March 2017).

On January 23, 2018, he sent the bitcoin-dev list the message “Taproot: Privacy preserving switchable scripting,” which proposes hiding an alternative script in an ordinary public key. BIP-0341 cites this proposal and Maxwell's direct contribution to its drafting. In 2019, he was one of the coauthors of the Erlay paper, with Gleb Naumenko, Pieter Wuille, Alexandra Fedorova, and Ivan Beschastnikh.

The dictionary links him to coinjoin, Blockstream, Confidential Transactions, Taproot, Bech32 and Bech32m, and Erlay.
