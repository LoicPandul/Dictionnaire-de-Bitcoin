Facility where many mining machines (often ASICs) are grouped together to mine bitcoin by taking part in the proof-of-work process. The purpose of this grouping is to make the fleet of machines easier to manage and to achieve economies of scale, notably in setup, maintenance, cooling, power supply, and network connection. A mining farm also improves the proximity of the miners, and thus reduces the latency that could affect their profitability.

*Not to be confused with a mining pool.*
