Liquidity marketplace for the Lightning Network developed by Amboss Technologies. Magma lets node operators buy or sell inbound capacity in the form of payment channels, regardless of the implementation used (LND, CLN, Eclair).

The mechanism relies on HODL invoices to create conditional contracts: the seller receives payment only if they open the agreed channel within a short time frame. This system allows Magma to facilitate trades without ever taking custody of users' funds.

Magma addresses a recurring need on the Lightning Network: obtaining inbound liquidity. A node that has just opened a channel has only outbound capacity; to receive payments, it must convince a peer to allocate funds on their side of the channel. Magma turns this process into an open market where liquidity is traded for fees negotiated between the parties.
