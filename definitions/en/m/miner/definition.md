Person who runs a mining machine, that is, a computer engaged in mining, the process of taking part in the proof of work. The miner groups the pending transactions in the mempool into a candidate block. The miner then searches for a valid hash of this block's header, lower than or equal to the target, by varying the nonces. On finding a valid hash, the miner broadcasts the block to the Bitcoin network and collects the associated monetary reward, made up of the block subsidy (the creation of new bitcoin out of thin air) and the transaction fees.

*In the specific context of mining pools, the role of miner is sometimes distinguished from the role of hasher, since the individual hashers who take part in the pooling only have to hash and do not take part in the full mining process.*
