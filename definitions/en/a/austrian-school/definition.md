School of economic thought that theorizes the market as a set of voluntary individual interactions, stresses the spontaneity of economic order, and criticizes state intervention. The Austrian School defends the role of private property, freedom of contract, and free trade, while criticizing the disruptive effects of money creation on the economy. Its contributors, such as Carl Menger, Ludwig von Mises, and Friedrich Hayek, worked on concepts such as price formation, the function of money, the dynamics of capital, and the subjective theory of value. The Austrian School criticizes socialism for its inability to carry out efficient economic calculation, and favors a classical liberal approach. It values the free market and sees state interventionism as a source of economic imbalances. Many bitcoiners subscribe to these ideas and consider Bitcoin a tool in harmony with this philosophy because of its distributed nature, the limit on its money creation, and its ability to operate independently of state intervention.
