Operation that combines two Taproot Assets of the same type into one within a single Taproot output. In an asset merge, assets coming from separate Taproot outputs are grouped into a single Merkle-Sum Sparse Merkle Tree committed in a new transaction. The total sum at the root of the tree then reflects all the merged assets. This operation is the inverse of an asset split. It consolidates balances that were fragmented by several receipts and simplifies the management of a holder's asset tree.
