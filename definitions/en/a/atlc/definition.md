Acronym for *Anchor Time Locked Contracts*. Conditional payment used in the Ark protocol to provide an atomic payment schedule to a single hub (the Ark service provider), thanks to connectors that form what is called a "txlock". The purpose of an ATLC is roughly the same as that of an HTLC on Lightning.

The principle comes from the initial Ark proposal, published by Burak Keceli on May 22, 2023, on the bitcoin-dev mailing list. An ATLC is attached in advance to each VTXO, the way an eltoo trigger is attached to a funding output. When the VTXO is spent, the ATLC attaches to a connector: an output of the round transaction that creates the new VTXOs. This link forms the "txlock", a condition that unlocks only if the agreed transaction actually exists. The service provider can therefore claim the ATLC only if this round transaction has not been replaced by another: it cannot double spend it, and the payment is settled in full or not at all.
