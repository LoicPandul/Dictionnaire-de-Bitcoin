Selection principle by which a miner chooses a transaction not solely on the basis of its own transaction fees, but also takes into account the fees of its ancestor transactions (its unconfirmed ancestors). This principle is what makes Child Pays For Parent (CPFP) possible: a child transaction with high fees can get its low-fee parent confirmed. Bitcoin Core has applied it to the construction of block templates since version 0.13.0, and generalizes it with the cluster mempool redesign of version 31.0, where selection is made by groups of transactions (chunks).
