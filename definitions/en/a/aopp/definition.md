Acronym for *Address Ownership Proof Protocol*. Controversial protocol designed to automatically prove ownership of Bitcoin addresses. This mechanism lets users demonstrate that they control a specific address, directly through their compatible wallet software. Initially, AOPP was created to simplify the verification of address ownership, a legal requirement for customers who want to move their bitcoin off exchanges in certain jurisdictions, such as Switzerland.

However, the protocol drew significant criticism within the Bitcoin community, because it could set a precedent in which users would have to ask for permission to exercise their right of ownership over their own funds (self-custody). In the face of this criticism, many wallet software projects chose not to adopt the protocol.
