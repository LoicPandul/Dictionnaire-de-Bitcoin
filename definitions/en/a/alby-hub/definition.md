Open-source application that runs a personal Lightning Network node and connects it to various applications through the Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC) protocol. Alby Hub installs on a personal server, a computer, or in the cloud, and serves as a control center for the user's Lightning payments. Alby Hub is non-custodial: the user keeps their private keys and full control of their funds. It uses an LDK (Lightning Development Kit) implementation to manage the Lightning node.
