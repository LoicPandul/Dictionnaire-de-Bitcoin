Developer, first lead maintainer of Bitcoin Core after Satoshi Nakamoto, and author of BIP-0011, BIP-0016, BIP-0034, and BIP-0101.

In late 2010, Satoshi Nakamoto, who was ceasing to post, handed him the code repository, and Gavin Andresen took over leadership of the project. In October 2011, he was assigned the number for BIP-0011, which defines M-of-N multisig transactions as standard transactions (`bare multisig`). In January 2012, he received the number for BIP-0016, which introduces P2SH: its rules apply to blocks dated from April 1, 2012. In July 2012, he received the number for BIP-0034, which requires the block height to be recorded in the coinbase transaction.

He is one of the founders of the Bitcoin Foundation, announced in September 2012. On April 7, 2014, he handed over maintenance to Wladimir van der Laan. In June 2015, he proposed BIP-0101, which makes the maximum block size grow over time; the code was merged into Bitcoin XT on August 6, 2015, then removed, and it remains one of the episodes of the Blocksize War.

On May 2, 2016, he wrote on his blog that he was convinced Craig Wright is Satoshi Nakamoto. His access to the Bitcoin Core GitHub organization was removed that same day; Wladimir van der Laan explained the reasons on his blog on May 6.
