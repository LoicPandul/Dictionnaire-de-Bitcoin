Set of regulatory measures to combat money laundering and the financing of terrorism (AML/CFT), adopted to prevent the financial system, including Bitcoin-related services, from being used for illegal activities. These measures include identifying and verifying the identity of customers (KYC), monitoring transactions to detect “suspicious” patterns, and cooperating with the authorities to report activities considered illegal. Regulated exchanges are required to comply with them in order to operate in many jurisdictions, notably France.
