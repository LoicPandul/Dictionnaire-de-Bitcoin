Upgrade of the `addr` message on the Bitcoin network, proposed in BIP-0155. The `addr` message was used to relay the addresses of nodes that accept inbound connections, but it was limited to 128-bit addresses. That size was adequate for IPv6, IPv4, and Tor v2 addresses, but insufficient for other protocols. The updated version, `addrv2`, is designed to support longer addresses, notably 256-bit Tor v3 hidden services, as well as other network protocols such as I2P or future ones.
