Privacy-focused mobile Bitcoin wallet, born from a fork of the Samourai Wallet code after the seizure of Samourai's servers by US authorities in April 2024. Ashigaru takes over the privacy protection features of Samourai, notably Whirlpool coinjoins, Stonewall transactions, collaborative transactions (Stowaway, StonewallX2), ricochets, and advanced UTXO control. The project is maintained by an open-source community that continues the development of these privacy tools.
