Optional field reserved in the witness structure of SegWit v1 (Taproot) inputs, defined by BIP-0341. The annex occupies the last position in the witness stack and is identified by a `0x50` prefix. Although in 2026 it has no function assigned by the consensus rules, this space was provided from the design of Taproot to accommodate future protocol extensions. As long as no semantics are defined for it, Bitcoin Core considers transactions that contain an annex nonstandard and does not relay them; BIP-0341 advises against including one, at the risk of permanently losing the funds.

An important property of the annex is that any Taproot or Tapscript signature must commit to its value when it is present. This means that an attacker cannot add, modify, or remove an annex without invalidating all the signatures of the input concerned. This cryptographic guarantee ensures that the annex cannot be exploited to alter a transaction that has already been signed.
