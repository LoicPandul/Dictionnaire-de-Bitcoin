Company specializing in Lightning payment solutions. Alby offers an ecosystem of complementary products:
* Alby Hub, a self-custodial Lightning node that can be self-hosted, deployed in the cloud, or used as an interface to a conventional node;
* the Alby browser extension, which lets users make Lightning payments and authenticate on websites directly from the browser;
* Alby Go, a mobile app for managing payments on the move.

Alby also provides APIs and the Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC) protocol so that developers can integrate Lightning payments into third-party applications.
