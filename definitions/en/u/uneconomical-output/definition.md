Transaction output whose amount is extremely small, to the point that the fees needed to spend it would be higher than its value. The precise definition of "dust" can vary depending on the context, but it generally refers to any transaction output that requires more fees to spend than the value it holds. For Bitcoin users, it is important to manage their UTXOs and to practice consolidation, so that they do not become dust.

*The term "uneconomical output" is sometimes used as a synonym for dust.*
