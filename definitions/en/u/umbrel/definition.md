Operating system for personal servers that makes it possible to self-host applications, including a Bitcoin node and a Lightning node. Umbrel offers a "plug-and-play" web interface that simplifies installing and managing a full node, with no need for advanced technical skills.

Umbrel runs on dedicated hardware (Umbrel Home, Umbrel Pro) or on any compatible computer via umbrelOS. Its app store includes dozens of self-hosted applications: Bitcoin Core, LND, Core Lightning, Nextcloud, Tailscale, Nostr relays, and others.
