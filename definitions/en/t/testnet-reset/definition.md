Operation that restarts Bitcoin's test network with a new genesis block, erasing the existing history and balances. This mechanism ensures that test coins stay easy to mine and without market value, which is important for the testnet to fulfill its function: providing a free experimentation environment where developers can test attacks, bugs, or new features without financial consequences.

The testnet has been reset three times in the history of Bitcoin. The first testnet was introduced in October 2010. A first reset was carried out in February 2011, creating what would later be called "testnet2". Gavin Andresen then launched "testnet3" in April 2012. After 13 years of service, testnet3 was finally replaced by testnet4 in 2024, defined in BIP-0094, notably because its coins had started to acquire market value.
