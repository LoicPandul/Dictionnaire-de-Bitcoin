Acronym for *Transparent Index Of Distinct Extended Shares*. Method for calculating miner payouts in mining pools, introduced by the Ocean pool in 2023. This method distributes rewards according to a weighted percentage of the work contributed to the most recently found shares. Each share is paid several times, with a reward calculation that includes transaction fees. This system ensures high precision in miner payments without needing a custodial intermediary to process them, unlike other methods such as FPPS. TIDES is designed for transparent payouts.
