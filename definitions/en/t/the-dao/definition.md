Project launched in 2016 on Ethereum that aimed to create an autonomous investment fund run by smart contracts. Although mainly tied to Ethereum, The DAO had a significant impact on the evolution of Bitcoin. In June 2016, The DAO fell victim to a massive hack. In response, the Ethereum community carried out a hard fork at block 1,920,000, which moved the diverted funds to a withdrawal contract. Part of the community rejected this change and continued the old chain, which became Ethereum Classic. This incident consolidated the conservative positions of the small blockers during Bitcoin's Blocksize War. They used the event to argue against rapid changes to the Bitcoin protocol, such as those advocated by the big blockers, and against the idea of carrying out hard forks, as was done on Ethereum following the hack of The DAO.
