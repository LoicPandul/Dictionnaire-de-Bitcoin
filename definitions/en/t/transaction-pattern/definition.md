Model, or typical overall structure, of a transaction, found on the blockchain and whose likely interpretation is known, which is useful in chain analysis. A pattern study focuses on a single transaction analyzed at a high level (unlike the internal and external heuristics of chain analysis). In other words, only the number of UTXOs as inputs and the number of UTXOs as outputs are examined, without dwelling on more specific details or on the transaction's environment. From the observed pattern, the nature of the transaction can be interpreted. Characteristics of its structure are looked for, and a likely interpretation is deduced from them.
