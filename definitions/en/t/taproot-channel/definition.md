Lightning channel anchored in a Taproot (P2TR) output rather than a conventional SegWit (P2WSH) output. Taproot channels take advantage of the improvements brought by the Taproot soft fork, activated on Bitcoin in November 2021.

When closed cooperatively, a Taproot channel is more efficient and more private than a traditional SegWit channel: the closing transaction looks like an ordinary Taproot spend through the key path, indistinguishable from a simple on-chain payment. This property strengthens the privacy of Lightning Network users.
