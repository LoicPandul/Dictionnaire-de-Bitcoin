Protocol developed by Lightning Labs for issuing assets on the main Bitcoin blockchain, taking advantage of the Taproot upgrade. Taproot Assets allows the creation of fungible assets such as stablecoins and non-fungible assets such as NFTs. Taproot Assets can be transferred through ordinary Bitcoin transactions or over the Lightning Network. The protocol uses Merkle-Sum Sparse Merkle Trees (MS-SMT), a kind of combination of MSTs and SMTs, to ensure the validity and auditability of assets.

*Taproot Assets Protocol used to be called “TARO”.*
