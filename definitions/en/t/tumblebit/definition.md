Anonymous payment hub concept compatible with Bitcoin, proposed in 2016 by Ethan Heilman, Leen AlShenibr, Foteini Baldimtsi, Alessandra Scafuro, and Sharon Goldberg. TumbleBit is a bitcoin mixing system that does not require trusting an intermediary. It lets users make fast, anonymous, off-chain payments through a coordinator called the Tumbler. TumbleBit guarantees anonymity by ensuring that even the Tumbler cannot link a payer's payment to its beneficiary. The TumbleBit protocol ensures that the Tumbler can neither steal bitcoin nor print fake bitcoin by issuing payments to itself. The anonymity offered by TumbleBit is comparable to that of a Chaum eCash system. However, the concept was never widely adopted, as privacy techniques such as Chaumian coinjoin were preferred to it.
