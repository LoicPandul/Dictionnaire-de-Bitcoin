Bitcoin developer who goes by Peter Todd. The author dates of his commits in Bitcoin Core run from April 2012 to April 2025 (the last one was merged in June 2025).

In September 2014, he wrote the implementation of `OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY`, which replaces the `OP_NOP2` opcode and can block the spending of an output until a given date or block height. His draft of BIP-0065 is dated October 1, 2014, and the number appeared in the BIP repository on November 10, 2014; the opcode code was merged into Bitcoin Core on June 26, 2015, and the soft fork activation logic on October 23, 2015.

He then coauthored BIP-0009, on version bits, with Pieter Wuille, Greg Maxwell, and Rusty Russell (September 2015), and BIP-0125 with David A. Harding (draft of December 2015). The latter BIP defines the signaling that a transaction may be replaced by another that pays more fees, Replace-by-Fee (RBF), and the rules for that replacement; since Bitcoin Core 28.0, replacement no longer requires this signal by default.

On September 15, 2016, he announced the public alpha of OpenTimestamps, which he initiated, a timestamping protocol that anchors hashes in the Bitcoin blockchain.

On October 8, 2024, the documentary "Money Electric: The Bitcoin Mystery" by Cullen Hoback, broadcast on HBO, put forward the claim that he is Satoshi Nakamoto. He publicly denied it. No evidence establishes it.
