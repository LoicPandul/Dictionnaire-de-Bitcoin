Adjustable parameter that determines how demanding the proof of work must be to add a new block to the blockchain and earn the associated reward. Difficulty is represented by the difficulty target, a 256-bit value that sets the upper limit the hash of a block header must respect to be considered valid. The goal is for the hash, computed by running `SHA256` twice (`SHA256d`), to be less than or equal to this target. To reach such a hash, miners vary the `nonce` in the block header. The difficulty adjusts every 2,016 blocks, or about every two weeks, to keep the average block time at about 10 minutes.
