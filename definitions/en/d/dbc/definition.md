Acronym for *Deterministic Bitcoin Commitments*. It is a set of rules for inscribing a commitment in a Bitcoin transaction in a provable and unique way. In the RGB protocol, there are two main forms of DBC:
* `Opret`;
* `Tapret`.

These mechanisms define precisely how the commitment is encoded in the outputs or in the structure of a Bitcoin transaction, to ensure that the commitment can be located and verified deterministically.
