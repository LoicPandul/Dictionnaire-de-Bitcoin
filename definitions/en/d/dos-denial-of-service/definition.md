Computer attack that aims to make a resource (website, node, online service, etc.) unavailable to its legitimate users. Attackers overload the target with an excessively high volume of data or requests, which exhausts the victim's system and network resources and causes slowdowns or a complete outage. DoS methods can vary, but the goal remains the same: to prevent access to services or data. In the specific context of Bitcoin, a DoS attack can aim to saturate the network or nodes with an excessive volume of requests to hinder their normal operation. The goal is often to harm a node operator or the network's availability for honest users.
