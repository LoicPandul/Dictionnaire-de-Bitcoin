Bitcoin developer, author of BIP-0022, BIP-0023, and BIP-0002, BIP editor from 2016 to 2026, and maintainer of Bitcoin Knots. He signs as Luke Dashjr, and sometimes Luke-Jr.

In 2011, he launched the Eligius mining pool, announced on April 27. In February 2012, he was assigned BIP-0022 and BIP-0023, which define `getblocktemplate`: the node supplies a block template that the mining software or the pool completes.

On October 22, 2014, he was one of the nine authors of the paper on pegged sidechains. On January 7, 2016, a commit of his own named him BIP editor, a role he held until August 9, 2026, the date of the commit by Mark Erhardt that removed him from the list of editors (merged on August 10). On February 3, 2016, BIP-0002 was assigned to him: it revises the BIP process. In 2017, he co-wrote BIP-0008 with Shaolin Fry, on version bits with lock-in by height.

He is the lead maintainer of Bitcoin Knots, a variant of Bitcoin Core that offers other transaction relay rules and, since version 29.4.2 of May 2026, consensus rules that Bitcoin Core does not enforce. In 2023, he cofounded Ocean, a mining pool whose miners can build their own block templates. The company behind it announced a seed round led by Jack Dorsey on November 28, 2023.
