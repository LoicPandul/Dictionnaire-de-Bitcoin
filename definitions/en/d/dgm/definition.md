Acronym for *Double Geometric Method*. Method for calculating miner payouts in the context of mining pools. DGM is a hybrid method, meant to combine the advantages of PPLNS with those of the so-called geometric method. Like PPLNS, it has low variance on shares, and it then lets the pool operator absorb part of the variance risk in a second stage. DGM resists pool hopping by guaranteeing that the expected payout per share stays constant. The method is based on scores, which makes payouts independent of the pool's history and almost entirely independent of future difficulty changes.
