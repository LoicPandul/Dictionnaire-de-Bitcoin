Situation in which the value of a digital asset, typically an IOU, becomes decoupled from the underlying asset to which it is normally pegged, causing price fluctuations relative to that asset. This phenomenon is especially observed with stablecoins, designed to maintain a strict parity with fiat currencies or other reference assets. A depeg can also occur with representations of bitcoin on sidechains or ETFs, where, in case of a problem, the nominal value may diverge from that of bitcoin.
