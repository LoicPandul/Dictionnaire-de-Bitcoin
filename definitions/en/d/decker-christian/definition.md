Researcher and later developer of Bitcoin layer 2 protocols, coauthor of duplex micropayment channels, of the eltoo paper, and of BIP-0118, and a major contributor to Core Lightning.

In 2013, at ETH Zurich, he published with Roger Wattenhofer a study on the propagation of information in the Bitcoin network. In 2015, the two researchers presented "A Fast and Scalable Payment Network with Bitcoin Duplex Micropayment Channels," a construction of bidirectional payment channels which, linked together, form a payment network.

On April 30, 2018, he proposed on the bitcoin-dev list a first version of `SIGHASH_NOINPUT`, which entered the BIP repository on May 21, 2018, under number 118. It became BIP-0118 `SIGHASH_ANYPREVOUT` after a rewrite by Anthony Towns in 2021, and remains in Draft status. The same day, he published with Rusty Russell (Blockstream) and Olaoluwa Osuntokun (Lightning Labs) the paper "eltoo: A Simple Layer2 Protocol for Bitcoin." Eltoo replaces the old states of a channel with state numbers that the chain enforces, but it requires this new signature flag.

He also contributes to the Core Lightning code: in 2026, cdecker is the second contributor to the repository, with 2,687 contributions.

His work connects several dictionary entries: payment channel, eltoo, BIP-0118, SIGHASH_ANYPREVOUT, Core Lightning, and Blockstream.
