Script that defines the specific conditions that inputs must meet to unlock the funds tied to a P2SH output. In a P2SH UTXO, the `scriptPubKey` contains the hash of the `redeemScript`. When a transaction wants to spend this UTXO as an input, it must provide the `redeemScript` in the clear, matching the hash contained in the `scriptPubKey`. The `redeemScript` is therefore given in the input's `scriptSig`, in addition to the other elements needed to satisfy the script's conditions, such as signatures or public keys. This encapsulated structure ensures that the details of the spending conditions stay hidden until the bitcoin is actually spent. It is notably used for legacy P2SH multisig wallets.
