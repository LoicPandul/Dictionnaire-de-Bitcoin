Decentralized and confidential smart contract system designed to work with Bitcoin and the Lightning Network. RGB operates on a client-side validation model and separates the storage of contract state from the blockchain, keeping only cryptographic commitments on it. The full history of states is thus maintained off-chain, which allows better scalability and privacy. RGB makes it possible to create complex contracts to issue tokens, NFTs, decentralized identities, or DeFi solutions, directly on top of Bitcoin.

On RGB, double-spend resistance is ensured by single-use seals, a cryptographic mechanism that takes advantage of the fact that UTXOs on Bitcoin can be spent only once. As for the authenticity of tokens, it is guaranteed by client-side verification of the state history, from the creation of the contract to its most recent state.
