Action, in an RGB State Extension, of claiming or exercising a previously declared Valency. The Valency, as a public right recorded in the contract state, gives an authorized participant the ability to claim a specific extension of that state. This mechanism thus formalizes the exercise of specific rights defined in the contract schema, ensuring that only the legitimately entitled party can initiate the corresponding transition and use the associated resources. Valencies and Redeems exist in RGB up to version 0.11; version 0.12.0 of rgb-core, released on July 10, 2025, removes them.
