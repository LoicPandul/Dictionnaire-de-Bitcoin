In the context of proof of work on the Bitcoin network, tool whose main function is to relay the new valid blocks that have been found. A distinction is sometimes drawn between the role of the miner, who is involved in the entire proof-of-work process, from building the candidate block to propagating the validated block, and the role of the hasher, who only takes part in hashing a block without any say in its selection or propagation. The role of relay, for its part, is to propagate the new blocks found by a hasher. In mining pools, the pool itself fills this role. It is also this role that gives the pool so much power over individual hashers. The term "relayer" can also refer to the person or entity that operates a relay.
