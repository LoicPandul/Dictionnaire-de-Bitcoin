American Bitcoin mining company listed on the NASDAQ under the symbol RIOT. Founded in 2000 in the biotechnology sector, it pivoted to Bitcoin mining in 2017 under the name Riot Blockchain, before being renamed Riot Platforms in 2023. The company owns and operates mining facilities in Texas and Kentucky, and ranks among the largest Bitcoin mining companies in the world by hashrate. Riot also has engineering and electrical infrastructure manufacturing capabilities through its subsidiary ESS Metron. The company is diversifying into hosting high-performance data centers for third-party customers.
