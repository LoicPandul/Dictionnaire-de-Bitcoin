Cryptographer and developer, coauthor of several cryptography and networking specifications for Bitcoin.

In 2014, he published *CoinShuffle* with Pedro Moreno-Sanchez and Aniket Kate, a protocol for mixing coins among users, without a trusted third party, compatible with Bitcoin as it exists; the paper appeared at the ESORICS conference. On January 19, 2020, he obtained, with Pieter Wuille and Jonas Nick, the number for BIP-0340, which defines Schnorr signatures on secp256k1 and serves as the basis for Taproot. In 2023, he obtained, with Jonas Nick and Elliott Jin, the number for BIP-0327, which specifies MuSig2, a multisignature scheme compatible with BIP-0340 (number assigned on March 26, 2023). He is also one of the four authors of BIP-0324, the version 2 encrypted P2P transport, with Dhruv Mehta, Jonas Schnelli, and Pieter Wuille; the draft dates from March 2019, and Bitcoin Core 26.0 includes initial support for it, disabled by default (enabled by default since 27.0).

In Bitcoin Core, he has 11 commits between 2018 and 2025. Two simplify the v2 transport in September 2023: one removes the garbage authentication packet from the handshake, the other simplifies the receive logic. Another updates the BIP-0324 test vectors in October 2025.

All three BIPs have Deployed status as of October 24, 2026.
