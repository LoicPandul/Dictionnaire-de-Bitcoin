Form of double spend that exploits the propagation delay of transactions across the Bitcoin network. The attacker simultaneously sends two transactions that spend the same UTXOs: one to a merchant to pay for a good or service, and the other to an address the attacker controls. The merchant, if they accept the transaction without waiting for a confirmation in a block, risks delivering the good while it is the second transaction that ends up confirmed by miners. This attack therefore targets exclusively payments accepted with “zero confirmation” (zero-conf). To guard against it, a merchant can wait for at least one confirmation before considering the payment valid, or use double-spend detection techniques by actively monitoring the network.
