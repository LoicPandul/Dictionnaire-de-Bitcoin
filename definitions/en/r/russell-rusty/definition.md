Developer of Core Lightning, one of the implementations of the Lightning Network, and top contributor to the BOLT specifications. He signs as Rusty Russell.

His first commit in the Core Lightning repository, which would be called c-lightning from 2017 on, is dated May 26, 2015. He worked on this software at Blockstream, and remains by far its top contributor, ahead of Christian Decker. On June 9, 2026, Blockstream published a post on his eleven years of work on Lightning, as he left the company.

In the lightning/bolts repository, which holds the protocol specifications, he is also the top contributor, with 382 commits since November 2016. From 2020, he led the drafting of BOLT-12, which describes reusable payment offers. Its sixth draft was merged on September 24, 2024: it was the first new BOLT since 2017, as the Blockstream post points out, citing Lisa Neigut.

His name appears among the authors of BIP-0009, "Version bits with timeout and delay," with Pieter Wuille, Peter Todd, and Gregory Maxwell. The BIP header dates the number assignment to October 4, 2015. This BIP reads the version field of the block header as a set of bits, each tracking an independent soft fork, so that several can be deployed in parallel. Its status is Deployed.

On March 25, 2026, the numbers BIP-0440 and BIP-0441 were assigned to Rusty Russell and Julian Moik, as part of the Great Script Restoration. BIP-0440 defines a varops budget, which extends the sigops budget of BIP-0342 to operations other than signatures. BIP-0441 introduces a Taproot leaf version, `0xc2`, which restores the opcodes disabled in August 2010, in Bitcoin version 0.3.10. Both BIPs are drafts.
