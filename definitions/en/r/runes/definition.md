Protocol developed by the same team as Ordinals that uses Bitcoin transactions to etch, mint, and transfer fungible digital assets other than BTC. Unlike Ordinals inscriptions, where each unit is unique, each rune is identical to the others, and therefore interchangeable, which makes it possible to create fungible cryptocurrencies other than BTC on Bitcoin. Messages of the Runes protocol, called runestones, are stored in `OP_RETURN` outputs.
