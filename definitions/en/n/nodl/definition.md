Company founded in late 2018 that designs turnkey Bitcoin and Lightning Network nodes, in the form of ready-to-use hardware boxes. Nodl was the first manufacturer to integrate BTCPay Server, to enforce Tor by default on all of its devices, and to offer full disk encryption with a physical security switch (kill switch). The company also offers nodl Cloud, an infrastructure service hosted in its own data centers (Paris, Geneva, Zurich) for individuals and businesses that want to run nodes without managing hardware.
