Open-source solo Bitcoin mining project that runs on ESP32 microcontrollers. Created by BitMaker in 2022, it lets users assemble a small, very low-power miner to see concretely how mining works. The device can be connected to a solo mining pool such as Public Pool. With a hashrate of about 78 kH/s, the odds of finding a block are astronomically low, but the project is above all educational and fun. NerdMiner shows real-time statistics on hashing and the Bitcoin network on its built-in screen.
