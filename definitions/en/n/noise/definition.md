Cryptographic framework used to establish and authenticate connections between Lightning nodes. The Noise protocol, specified in BOLT-08, defines the initial handshake during which two nodes exchange their keys and negotiate an encrypted session. It relies on a Diffie-Hellman key exchange with ephemeral and static keys, to provide forward secrecy. Once the handshake is complete, all communications between the two nodes are encrypted and authenticated. Noise replaces the TLS or SSH protocols usually used in network applications, with a simpler specification better suited to the constraints of the Lightning Network.
