Open and decentralized communication protocol, whose acronym stands for *Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays*. Created by fiatjaf in 2020, Nostr relies on a simple architecture: clients cryptographically sign events (the protocol's single data structure) and publish them to relays, servers that store and redistribute these events. A user's identity is a cryptographic key pair on the secp256k1 curve, the same one used by Bitcoin. No central server controls access or content: if a relay censors a user, that user can migrate to other relays without losing their identity or their audience. Nostr is closely tied to the Bitcoin ecosystem, notably through the native integration of zaps (Lightning micropayments) and the use of the same cryptographic infrastructure. The protocol is extensible thanks to NIPs (*Nostr Implementation Possibilities*), community specifications that define event formats and features. Beyond microblogging, Nostr serves as the foundation for a variety of applications: encrypted messaging, long-form articles, marketplaces, closed groups, and even code collaboration through Git.
