Private meeting held in 2017, bringing together more than 50 companies from the Bitcoin ecosystem, following the Consensus 2017 conference. The purpose of this meeting was to put an end to the long-running debate on Bitcoin's scaling by reaching an agreement. The SegWit2x proposal came out of this meeting, drawing on the earlier SegWit2Mb proposal. It provided for two major changes to the Bitcoin protocol:
* The adoption of SegWit with an activation threshold set at 80% of signaling;
* A hard fork intended to raise the maximum block size from 1 MB to 2 MB.

Despite positive signaling from more than 80% of miners, the project failed to rally enough consensus, which led to its abandonment. This event was interpreted by many users and developers as an attempt to attack Bitcoin.
