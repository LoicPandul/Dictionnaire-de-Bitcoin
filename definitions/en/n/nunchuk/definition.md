Bitcoin wallet software specialized in multisig, available on desktop and mobile (iOS, Android). Nunchuk makes it easier to create and manage multisig wallets by offering an accessible interface, even for non-technical users. The app supports most hardware wallets on the market and uses PSBTs to coordinate signatures between several devices. Nunchuk also offers collaborative features that let several people cosign transactions asynchronously. The software includes assisted recovery and inheritance options. It comes in a free version with the basic features and a paid version with additional services such as assisted multisig.
