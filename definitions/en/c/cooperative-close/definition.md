Closing of a Lightning channel carried out with the mutual agreement of both parties. It is the most advantageous closing method: the two channel partners jointly sign a closing transaction that redistributes the funds to their respective addresses, without a timelock.

Unlike a force close, where a unilateral commitment transaction is broadcast and imposes a timelock before the funds become accessible, a cooperative close produces a simple transaction. Mining fees are therefore lower (since they can be adjusted to the current fee market), and the funds are available as soon as the transaction is confirmed.
