In the context of Bitcoin, the holding and management of the private keys that control bitcoin. Custody of BTC can be provided in two ways: either personally by the user, who keeps the private keys needed to access their bitcoin themselves (known as “self-custody”), or by a third party, such as an exchange, where the company holds the private keys and manages the bitcoin on the user's behalf. This second option is riskier than self-custody, because it exposes the user's funds to the risks of hacking, bankruptcy, or fraudulent behavior by the custodian.
