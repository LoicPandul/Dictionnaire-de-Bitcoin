Family of hardware wallets made by the Canadian company Coinkite, designed exclusively for Bitcoin. Coldcards stand out for their maximum-security approach: they are fully air-gapped, meaning they can work without ever being connected to a computer, exchanging transactions via a microSD card, NFC, or QR codes. Coldcards include secure elements to protect private keys against physical access. They offer many advanced security features, for example the "Brick Me PIN" (which destroys the data) and the "Duress PIN" (which opens a decoy wallet).

There are several different Coldcards:
* The MK subfamily (MK3, MK4), which has a numeric keypad only, a small screen, and no camera for scanning QR codes;
* The Q subfamily, the brand's top-of-the-line product, with a QR code scanner, a battery compartment, and a full QWERTY keyboard.
