Method proposed in 2012 by Yoni Assia, Vitalik Buterin, Lior Hakim, Meni Rosenfeld, and Rotem Lev for representing and managing non-native assets on the Bitcoin blockchain. The idea was to attach metadata to specific transactions, in order to "color" bitcoin to indicate its association with physical assets. Among the implementations, the Open Assets Protocol was specified by Flavien Charlon in December 2013. This protocol made it possible to mark bitcoin using `OP_RETURN`.
