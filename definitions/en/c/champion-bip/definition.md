In the BIP process, described by BIP-0002, the person who writes and takes charge of managing a BIP (Bitcoin Improvement Proposal). The champion is responsible for writing the BIP following the formal process, defending it before the community, answering questions, and guiding discussions to refine the proposal. They also work to obtain the consensus needed for the BIP to be accepted and implemented.

*The champion is sometimes also called the “author”. Their name appears after the “Author” header in BIPs. There can sometimes be several champions for the same BIP.*
