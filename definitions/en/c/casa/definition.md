American company offering multisig bitcoin security solutions for individuals. Casa provides a service in which private keys are split between the user (on their hardware wallet and phone) and Casa (which holds an emergency recovery key). This 2-of-3 or 3-of-5 model secures funds without a single point of failure, while keeping a recovery option in case a device is lost.

Casa also offers a built-in inheritance protocol. It is used through a mobile app, and the service runs on a subscription basis with different security tiers.
