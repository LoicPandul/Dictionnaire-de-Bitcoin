Bitcoin mixing protocol proposed in 2014 by Tim Ruffing, Pedro Moreno-Sanchez, and Aniket Kate, inspired by the idea formalized by Gregory Maxwell. CoinShuffle breaks the history of coins without requiring a trusted third party. The protocol ensures that no participant can link the inputs and outputs of the other participants. The concept was never widely adopted, as privacy techniques such as the Chaumian coinjoin were preferred to it.
