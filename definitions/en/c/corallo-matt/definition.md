Bitcoin Core developer, author of BIP-0152 on compact block relay and coauthor of BIP-0054 on consensus cleanup. He also goes by the pseudonym TheBlueMatt on GitHub.

In 2012, he co-wrote two specifications: BIP-0021, which defines the format of `bitcoin:` URIs, with Nils Schneider, and BIP-0037, on Bloom filtering of connections, with Mike Hearn. In 2016, he published BIP-0152 (Compact Block Relay), assigned on April 27, 2016: a node announces a block by short transaction identifiers, which the peer reconstructs from its mempool, reducing the bandwidth used to relay blocks. The implementation was merged into Bitcoin Core on June 22, 2016 (#8068).

On July 7, 2016, he introduced FIBRE (Fast Internet Bitcoin Relay Engine), a relay system based on UDP and error correction, which succeeds his Bitcoin Relay Network to speed up block propagation. He also launched Rust-Lightning, a Lightning library in Rust that Square Crypto, later renamed Spiral, subsequently took over to build LDK. Square Crypto announced his hiring on August 20, 2019.

In 2025, he co-wrote BIP-0054 with Antoine Poinsot, which proposes fixing several consensus weaknesses: the timewarp attack, the validation time of certain blocks, weaknesses of the Merkle tree, and duplicate transactions. The number was assigned on April 11, 2025.
