Acronym for "Contract Execution Transaction". Specific transaction within a DLC that allows final settlement between the parties based on the outcome of a future event. When the oracle publishes a signature matching the outcome of the event, the parties use this signature to complete and unlock the CET, which sends the funds to the winning party. The signed CET is then broadcast to the network, and the winner receives the bitcoin owed to them under the terms of the smart contract. All the other potential CETs, which would have been executed for different outcomes, become obsolete and are abandoned.
