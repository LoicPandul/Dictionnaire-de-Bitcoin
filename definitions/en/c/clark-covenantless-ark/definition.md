Version of the Ark protocol that can be implemented without requiring covenants to be added to Bitcoin through a soft fork. The name "clArk" is a contraction of "covenantless Ark". When Ark was first proposed in May 2023 by Burak Keceli, the protocol required covenants, which were not yet available on Bitcoin. The clArk version, developed starting in 2024, gets around this limitation.

Without covenants, clArk emulates the spending conditions by requiring all participants in a round to sign n-of-n multisig transactions. Each participant must sign the entire VTXO tree making up the round, whereas covenants would let them sign only the clauses concerning their own funds. This approach brings significant overhead in bandwidth, storage, and interactivity: all participants must be online at the same time during the round.

Adding covenants to Bitcoin would substantially improve the protocol: users would no longer need to be connected permanently, the ASPs' liquidity management would be optimized, and the protocol could better handle certain scenarios. clArk is thus a compromise that makes it possible to deploy Ark on Bitcoin as it exists today, without needing a soft fork.
