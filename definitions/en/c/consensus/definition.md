Mechanism by which all the nodes of the Bitcoin network come to agree on the shared state of the blockchain. Consensus lets all users align on a single history of Bitcoin transactions, notably to prevent double spending. Bitcoin's consensus mechanism is sometimes called “Nakamoto consensus”. It relies on proof of work and specifies that all nodes of the network accept the chain with the greatest amount of accumulated work.

*By extension, the unwritten rules of the Bitcoin protocol are sometimes also called “consensus”.*
