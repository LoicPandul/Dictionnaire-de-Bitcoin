Trust-minimized two-way bridge developed by Chainway Labs for the Citrea rollup. Clementine lets users transfer BTC between the main Bitcoin chain and Citrea (BTC ↔ cBTC) without a classic trusted multisig. The protocol relies on BitVM2 and works with a 1-of-N trust assumption: a single honest participant in the group of verifiers is enough for the funds to be protected against theft. For a deposit (peg-in), the user sends BTC to a Taproot address encoding two spending paths: one lets the signers move the funds to a vault, the other allows an automatic refund after a timelock if the bridge does not react. For a withdrawal (peg-out), the user burns their cBTC on Citrea, then an operator advances the funds in BTC on Bitcoin and is later reimbursed from the vault, provided that no challenger proves fraudulent behavior through a challenge mechanism using BitVM2. Clementine is open source.
