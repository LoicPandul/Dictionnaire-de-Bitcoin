Open-source wallet available on iOS, Android, macOS, Linux, and Windows, originally designed for Monero and later extended to Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies. CakeWallet stands out in the Bitcoin ecosystem for its privacy features, notably support for Silent Payments and Payjoin v2. The wallet is non-custodial and lets the user keep control of their private keys. It offers a built-in exchange service for converting between different cryptocurrencies, as well as native Tor integration to strengthen privacy at the network level.
