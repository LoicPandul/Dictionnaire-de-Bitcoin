Unique identifier of a Lightning channel on the Bitcoin blockchain. It consists of the txid of the channel's funding transaction followed by the index of the corresponding output, in the form `txid:output_index`. For example, `a3f2...b7c:0` designates the first output of the funding transaction. The channel point identifies the on-chain transaction that established the channel and serves as a reference in node administration commands, for example to close a specific channel or to look up its details.
