Central server that connects users who want to do coinjoins and coordinates the construction of the collaborative transactions, without being able to associate the inputs with the specific outputs of each user. This is possible thanks to the Tor network and to cryptographic mechanisms that vary by protocol: Chaum blind signatures in ZeroLink (Wasabi 1.x), keyed-verification anonymous credentials in WabiSabi (Wasabi 2.0). Unlike a traditional bitcoin mixer, the coinjoin coordinator never takes possession of the participants' bitcoin and is unable to associate inputs and outputs.
