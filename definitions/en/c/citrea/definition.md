First zero-knowledge proof rollup (ZK rollup) deployed on Bitcoin, developed by Chainway Labs. Citrea uses a Type 2 zkEVM, built with RISC Zero, which offers full compatibility with the Ethereum virtual machine and lets Solidity developers deploy their contracts directly on Bitcoin. The system processes transactions off-chain in batches, generates STARK proofs (then wrapped in SNARK proofs), and publishes these proofs directly on Bitcoin, which serves as both the data availability layer and the settlement layer. Citrea's two-way bridge, called Clementine, relies on BitVM2 and works with a 1-of-N trust assumption: as long as at least one verifier of the bridge is honest, funds are safe. Native bitcoin on Citrea is called cBTC. The platform also offers ctUSD, a dollar-pegged stablecoin backed by US Treasury bills, issued in partnership with MoonPay and M0. Citrea requires no change to Bitcoin's consensus rules. The mainnet has been live since January 2026.
