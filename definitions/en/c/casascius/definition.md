Physical coins that hold bitcoin, created from 2011 by Mike Caldwell. Each Casascius coin embeds a private key concealed under a tamper-evident hologram, which lets its holder access the associated bitcoin.

The coins came in several denominations (1 BTC, 10 BTC, 25 BTC, 100 BTC...) and bore a design with a golden “B”. They made it possible to transfer bitcoin physically, without going through the network, simply by handing the coin to the recipient.

In November 2013, the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) ruled that this activity was a form of money transmission requiring registration. Mike Caldwell then stopped selling preloaded coins, while continuing to produce blank ones. Casascius coins have become sought-after collector's items.
