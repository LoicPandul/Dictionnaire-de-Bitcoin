Software developed by Chris Belcher and launched in August 2014, designed to make coinjoins easier to use through a GUI. Unlike other coinjoin implementations of the time, which required participants to want to coinjoin at the same time, CoinJumble allowed the parts of a transaction to be shared asynchronously. Users could exchange over external communication channels to share the encoded parts of the transaction. Today, CoinJumble is no longer used.
