Message broadcast on the Lightning network to signal the existence of a new payment channel to other nodes. This message is what distinguishes a public channel from a private one: only channels whose partners choose to broadcast a channel announcement appear in the network graph and can be used by third parties to route payments.

The message contains notably the identifiers of the two partner nodes, the reference to the on-chain funding transaction, and the signatures of both parties. On receiving this message, the other nodes of the network check that the partners do hold the funds in the funding transaction before adding the channel to their local database. The channel announcement is part of the Lightning Network's gossip protocol, which ensures the propagation of information about the network topology between peers. Channels that do not broadcast this message are called "private" (or "unannounced") and remain invisible in the graph.
