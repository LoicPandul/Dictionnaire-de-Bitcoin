In Samourai Wallet and other wallet software that implements it, a Cahoot is any type of transaction carried out collaboratively by several users. Doing a Cahoot therefore means taking part jointly in a transaction. This collaboration revolves around the exchange of partially signed transactions. These exchanges can be done either manually, through QR codes, or automatically, through the Soroban communication network. Cahoots include:
* Stowaway transactions (Payjoin);
* Stonewall x2 transactions;
* Joinbot transactions.

Samourai Wallet's servers were seized after the arrest of its two cofounders on April 24, 2024, and the service has been unavailable since.
