Native representation of bitcoin on the Citrea rollup. cBTC (Citrea Bitcoin) is the native currency used to pay gas fees and make transactions on the Citrea network. It is obtained by depositing BTC on the main Bitcoin chain through the Clementine bridge, which locks the funds and issues an equivalent amount of cBTC on the rollup. It can be converted back to BTC at any time through Clementine's peg-out mechanism.
