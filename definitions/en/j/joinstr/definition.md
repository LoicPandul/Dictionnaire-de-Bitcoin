Decentralized coinjoin protocol that uses Nostr as its communication layer. Proposed in August 2022 by the pseudonymous developer "1440000bytes", it removes the need for a central coordinator, which has traditionally been a point of failure in coinjoin implementations. Participants register their outputs through Nostr relays, then the system creates a partially signed Bitcoin transaction (PSBT) that each participant signs individually. Once all the signatures are collected, the combined transaction is broadcast to the network.
