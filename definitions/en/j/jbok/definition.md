Acronym for *Just a Bunch Of Keys*. JBOK wallets are old Bitcoin wallets that stored a set of key pairs generated independently and pseudorandomly. Unlike modern HD wallets, which generate keys deterministically and hierarchically from a single seed, JBOK wallets had no hierarchical or deterministic relationship between keys. They were all independent of one another. Because of their less efficient management and the difficulty of backing them up, these wallets became obsolete and were naturally replaced by more advanced HD solutions, as standardized in BIP-0032.
