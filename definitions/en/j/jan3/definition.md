Company founded in 2022 by Samson Mow, former chief strategy officer of Blockstream. The name “JAN3” refers to January 3, 2009, the date on which Satoshi Nakamoto created Bitcoin's genesis block. Jan3 is dedicated to accelerating Bitcoin adoption at the nation-state level, building infrastructure based on layers such as the Lightning Network and on sidechains such as the Liquid Network. The company works notably with El Salvador and the autonomous region of Madeira to integrate Bitcoin into their financial systems. Jan3 is also active in software development, notably with the Aqua wallet.
