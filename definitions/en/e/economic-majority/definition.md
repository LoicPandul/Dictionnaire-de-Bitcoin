Largest proportion of the economic activity involving the bitcoin currency, generated by merchants. A merchant is any natural or legal person willing to exchange a good or service for BTC. These merchants, which include businesses, users, exchanges, and miners, vary in size and economic influence. Some are major players, generating substantial economic activity, while others are more modest. The economic majority is therefore defined by those whose combined economic activity represents the predominant share of this currency. This majority has influence over the consensus rules, notably in the event of a fork.
