Open-source blockchain platform for deploying federated sidechains pegged to Bitcoin or standalone blockchains. Launched in June 2015, Elements is built on the Bitcoin Core code and extends it with advanced features: Confidential Transactions (hidden amounts and asset types), Issued Assets (issuance of several asset types on the same chain), additional opcodes, and the Federated Peg (a two-way peg with Bitcoin through a federation of signers).

Consensus relies on blocks signed by a federation of participants with aligned interests, which eliminates the risk of multi-block reorganizations and speeds up transaction finality compared with proof-of-work mining. Liquid Network, the most widely used sidechain in the Bitcoin ecosystem, is the main production implementation of Elements.
