Highly sophisticated form of attack on the Bitcoin network that lets a malicious Internet service provider isolate specific Bitcoin nodes. It is therefore a form of eclipse attack. The Erebus attack exploits the structure of the Internet, in particular the mandatory chokepoints (or "bottlenecks") in routing between autonomous systems (ASes). By controlling an autonomous system, an attacker can manipulate network traffic to isolate a Bitcoin node from the rest of the network, and thereby make it believe in a false state of the blockchain (blocks or transactions not known to the node). This isolation can lead to double spends or censorship against the isolated node. The `-asmap` option, introduced with Bitcoin Core 0.20.0, makes this attack harder: the node limits the number of its connections to a single autonomous system. It remains disabled by default, including in Bitcoin Core 31.0, which ships the necessary data for the first time.
