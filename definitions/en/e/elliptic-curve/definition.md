In cryptography, an algebraic curve defined by an equation of the form $y^2 = x^3 + ax + b$. These curves are used in elliptic curve cryptography (ECC), a public-key cryptography method for building encryption, digital signature, and key exchange algorithms. In Bitcoin, the ECDSA (Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm) or Schnorr signatures are used with the `secp256k1` curve. This curve was chosen for its performance and security properties. These algorithms are used to generate public keys from private keys, as well as to sign transactions, and thus unlock bitcoin.
