Company that publishes Wasabi Wallet, an open-source desktop Bitcoin wallet focused on privacy. zkSNACKs developed the WabiSabi protocol, a coinjoin mechanism used in Wasabi Wallet 2.0 to improve the privacy of Bitcoin transactions. In June 2024, zkSNACKs ended its coinjoin coordination service because of regulatory uncertainty. Wasabi Wallet continues to work as a standard Bitcoin wallet, and other WabiSabi coinjoin coordinators have emerged.
