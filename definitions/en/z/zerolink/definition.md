Chaumian coinjoin protocol that aims to break all links between separate sets of coins through advanced mixing techniques. The ZeroLink protocol stands out for its ability to protect users' anonymity against various forms of chain analysis at the transaction and network levels. ZeroLink introduces a framework for coinjoin wallets, with the use of segregated premix and postmix accounts, as well as a mixing technique of its own: Chaumian coinjoin. The two main implementations of ZeroLink were Wasabi Wallet (version 1.0) and Whirlpool, available on Samourai Wallet and Sparrow Wallet. This protocol [was introduced by nopara73 and TDevD in 2017](https://github.com/nopara73/ZeroLink/blob/master/README.md).
