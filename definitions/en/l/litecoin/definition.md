Altcoin created in 2011 by Charlie Lee, a former Google engineer. Litecoin (LTC) is a fork of Bitcoin's source code with a few parameter changes: a block time reduced to 2.5 minutes instead of 10, a total supply capped at 84 million units (against 21 million for Bitcoin), and the Scrypt hashing algorithm instead of SHA-256 for proof of work. It has sometimes served as a testing ground for features later adopted by Bitcoin, such as SegWit, which was activated on Litecoin in May 2017 and on Bitcoin in August of the same year.
