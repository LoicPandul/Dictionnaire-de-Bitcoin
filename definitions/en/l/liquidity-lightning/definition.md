Capacity of a Lightning payment channel to route transactions between two nodes, based on the bitcoin available on each side of the channel. There are two types of liquidity: inbound and outbound. Outbound liquidity is the funds the node can send or forward through the channel. Inbound liquidity is the funds it can receive or forward in the opposite direction. Liquidity can also refer to the sum of the liquidity available across all of a node's channels.
