Swiss nonprofit association founded in 2019 by Maxim Orlovsky and Giacomo Zucco. The acronym LNP/BP stands for "Lightning Network Protocol / Bitcoin Protocol". The association is responsible for developing and maintaining the standards of the RGB protocol, a smart contract system that runs on Bitcoin and the Lightning Network using client-side validation. RGB enables the issuance and transfer of digital assets and smart contracts directly on Bitcoin, keeping the data off-chain to preserve privacy and scalability. The association publishes and maintains a set of technical standards (LNPBP) that govern how the protocol works.
