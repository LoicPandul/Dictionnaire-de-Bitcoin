Software development kit (SDK) for Lightning. LDK is a collection of libraries and tools that lets developers integrate Lightning into their software or build Lightning applications with less complexity. LDK handles the complex aspects of integrating Lightning-related features. Rust-Lightning (RL), on which LDK is built, was launched by Matt Corallo (first commit on December 25, 2017), who joined Square Crypto in August 2019. Square Crypto, an entity created by Jack Dorsey and later renamed Spiral, then developed LDK from this base.
