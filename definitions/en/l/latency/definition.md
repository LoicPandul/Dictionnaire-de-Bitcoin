Delay between the sending and the receiving of a piece of information. In computing, it is the time data takes to travel between two computers. In the context of Bitcoin, latency can be studied as the delay in propagating blocks across the network. High latency can put isolated miners at a disadvantage, because they keep working on blocks that new blocks have already made obsolete until they receive those new blocks. Latency therefore naturally pushes miners to group together. It also reduces the overall security of the system by wasting the search for a valid proof of work. The 10-minute interval between blocks is a measure meant to reduce the impact of latency on the system.
