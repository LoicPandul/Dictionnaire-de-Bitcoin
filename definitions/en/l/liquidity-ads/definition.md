Mechanism proposed for the Lightning Network protocol that lets node operators advertise their willingness to provide inbound liquidity in exchange for payment. A node that holds funds can publish an offer on the network, specifying the amount of liquidity it is willing to allocate, the duration of the commitment, and the rate it charges. When another node needs inbound capacity to receive payments, it can accept this offer and open a channel funded by the liquidity provider. This system creates an open, decentralized market for liquidity on Lightning, which solves a recurring problem for new nodes that cannot receive payments without inbound capacity. Liquidity ads remain a specification proposal: the first one (`option_will_fund`, BOLTs #878, opened in 2021 by Lisa Neigut) was closed without being merged, and an extensible version (BOLTs #1153, opened in 2024 by Bastien Teinturier) is still open as of October 2026. They rely on dual funding (`open_channel2`), which allows the channel opening to be negotiated, and are already implemented in Core Lightning and Eclair. This system automates negotiation between the parties without a trusted intermediary.
