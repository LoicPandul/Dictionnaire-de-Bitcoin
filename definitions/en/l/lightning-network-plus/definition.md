Online service that makes it easier to set up liquidity swaps on the Lightning Network. Several node operators coordinate to simultaneously open payment channels between each other in a circle, which guarantees that each of them receives as much inbound capacity as they offer. For example, three users A, B, and C agree on the LN+ website on a liquidity swap of 1M sats: A opens a 1M sat channel to B, B does the same to C, and C in turn opens a channel to A.

This system improves the connectivity of a Lightning node while providing as much inbound liquidity as the outbound liquidity committed.
