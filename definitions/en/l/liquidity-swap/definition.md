Mechanism for coordinated opening of Lightning channels in which several node operators commit to opening channels to one another in a circular arrangement. Each participant opens a channel to the next one in the circle, which guarantees that everyone gets both inbound and outbound liquidity. The concept is also known as a "ring of fire".

The LN+ platform is the most widely used service for organizing these swaps. On this platform, the participants, usually between three and five, agree on an identical channel capacity. Once all the channels are open, the circle forms a loop in which each node has improved connectivity to the network. A circular rebalance can then be performed to spread liquidity evenly across the channels.
