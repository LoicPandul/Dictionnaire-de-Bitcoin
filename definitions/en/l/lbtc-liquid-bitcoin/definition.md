Ticker symbol for bitcoin circulating on the Liquid Network, the federated sidechain developed by Blockstream. Each L-BTC is created through a two-way peg process (peg-in): BTC is locked on the main chain at an address controlled by the federation, and an equivalent amount of L-BTC is issued on Liquid. The reverse operation (peg-out) destroys the L-BTC on Liquid and releases the corresponding BTC on the main chain.
