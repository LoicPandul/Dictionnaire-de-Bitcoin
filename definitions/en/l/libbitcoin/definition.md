Set of libraries written in C++ for building Bitcoin-related applications. Libbitcoin provides independent building blocks for developing mobile applications, software, and other systems around Bitcoin, and its architecture is therefore modular. All of the project's libraries are distributed under the AGPL free software license.

Launched in 2011 by a team of developers led by Amir Taaki, Libbitcoin was then the second complete implementation of Bitcoin, after Satoshi's original client. Today, Eric Voskuil is the lead maintainer and the most active contributor to the project.
