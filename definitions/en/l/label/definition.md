Tag or annotation attached to a UTXO in a Bitcoin wallet to remember where it came from. For example, a UTXO received from a P2P purchase on Bisq with Charlie can be given the label “`Non-KYC Bisq Charles`”. This is a good practice that helps recall the origin or intended destination of the UTXO, which makes it easier to manage funds and optimize privacy. Labeling matters even more when combined with coin control. By letting users tell UTXOs apart and select them precisely for their transactions, it helps avoid merging UTXOs from different sources. This limits the risks associated with the chain analysis heuristic known as the common-input-ownership heuristic (CIOH), which can reveal that the inputs of a transaction share a common owner.
