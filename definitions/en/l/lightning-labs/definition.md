Company founded in 2016 by Elizabeth Stark and Olaoluwa Osuntokun, specializing in building infrastructure for the Lightning Network. Lightning Labs is the main maintainer of LND, one of the most widely used implementations of the Lightning protocol. The company also develops an ecosystem of complementary tools: Lightning Loop for submarine swaps, Lightning Pool for liquidity, Faraday for node analysis, Lightning Terminal for the graphical interface, and the Taproot Assets protocol for issuing assets on Bitcoin.
