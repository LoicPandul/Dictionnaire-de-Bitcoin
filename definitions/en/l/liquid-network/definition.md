Bitcoin sidechain designed by Blockstream to provide fast, confidential transactions. Unlike the main Bitcoin blockchain, Liquid uses a consensus mechanism based on a federation (a selected group of node operators, generally Bitcoin-related companies), which replaces Nakamoto consensus. This approach speeds up transactions considerably and reduces costs, while offering more advanced features. Liquid also allows the issuance of digital assets, including tokens that represent other cryptocurrencies. The bitcoin on Liquid, called L-BTC, is pegged to BTC through a two-way peg system run by part of the federation. The members of this federation are called "functionaries", and they can take on both the role of "watchman" and that of "blocksigner".
