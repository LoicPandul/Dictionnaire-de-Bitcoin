Bitcoin developer, first author of BIP-0141, the SegWit specification. He has contributed to Bitcoin Core since 2013, notably on support for multiple wallets in the graphical interface, and is among the contributors to version 0.13.1.

In 2015, he co-wrote BIP-0112, which introduces `OP_CHECKSEQUENCEVERIFY`, with BtcDrak and Mark Friedenbach, and wrote alone BIP-0123, which classifies BIPs by layer. In December 2015, he received the number for BIP-0141, which he signed with Johnson Lau and Pieter Wuille: SegWit moves signatures out of the transaction structure into a separate structure, the witness, which eliminates third-party malleability of transactions that spend SegWit outputs. He also co-wrote with Pieter Wuille BIP-0144, which adapts transaction relay between nodes. Bitcoin Core 0.13.1, released on October 27, 2016, includes the SegWit activation parameters.

However, activation by miner signaling, defined by BIP-0009, ran into the refusal of some of them. On June 17, 2017, Lombrozo published an article in which he argued that a minority of hash power holds a veto there, and that future upgrades will have to combine activation by miners with activation by users. He recommended running BIP-0148, proposed by Shaolin Fry, a UASF that requires miners to signal SegWit from August 1, 2017. He was subsequently cited, with Luke Dashjr, among the figures of this movement. SegWit activated at block 481,824, on August 24, 2017.

Asked in 2020 about the Taproot activation, he said that BIP-0148 had played an important role, and that Bitcoin Core should not deploy protocol changes on its own.
