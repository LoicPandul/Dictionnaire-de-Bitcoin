Role in the Bitcoin Core hierarchy, which has not existed since February 2023. The person holding it was in charge of leading the project and therefore had more power than the maintainers. The role of lead maintainer was naturally taken on by Satoshi Nakamoto until his departure in early 2011. After that, Gavin Andresen, who had already contributed alongside Satoshi, took over at the head of the software until early 2014. From then on, Wladimir J. van der Laan held the role until February 2023. Since then, the project has had no lead maintainer.
