Company cofounded by David Marcus, formerly head of PayPal and of the Libra/Diem project at Meta, with the mission of building open payments infrastructure on Bitcoin. Lightspark first developed services and tools to ease integration of the Lightning Network, notably the UMA (Universal Money Address) protocol, which enables interoperable transactions between institutions. The company then created Spark, a layer 2 protocol based on statechains, designed to overcome Lightning's limits in scaling self-custody wallets and supporting stablecoins. Lightspark also offers a Wallet-as-a-Service platform and stablecoin issuance built on Spark's capabilities.
