Company operating on Lightning whose business is providing liquidity services to users.

On Lightning, payments are made off-chain through bidirectional channels, whose capacity is limited by the funds locked in the on-chain 2-of-2 multisig. The main role of an LSP is to make this liquidity easier to manage, either by helping convert on-chain funds to Lightning, or by opening new channels to increase users' inbound capacity.

These services are often used by non-custodial wallets, so that the user can receive Lightning payments immediately without having to open and manage channels. In exchange, the LSP charges fees.
