Single-use USB device made by Coinkite, designed as a bearer asset for bitcoin. The Opendime generates a private key internally the first time it is used. This key is known to no one. Bitcoin can then be sent to the associated address. To spend the bitcoin secured by this key, the device must be physically unsealed by inserting a pin into a hole provided for that purpose on the circuit board. The private key is then revealed in WIF format, and the user can spend the associated funds. As long as the device stays sealed, it can be passed from hand to hand without creating an on-chain transaction. The physical seal makes it possible to verify with certainty, during an exchange, that the payer does not know the private key, simply by inspecting the device.
