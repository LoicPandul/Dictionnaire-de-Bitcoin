Exploratory analysis tool for the Bitcoin blockchain, created by LaurentMT and later acquired by Samourai Wallet. OXT offered advanced features for exploring and visualizing the chain, with a particular focus on studying transaction privacy.

The platform could display transaction graphs, compute privacy metrics such as the Boltzmann score, and identify usage patterns (coinjoins, consolidations, etc.). It was thus used to understand and audit the level of privacy provided by a transaction or a set of transactions.

The service, once freely accessible at oxt.me, has been offline since April 2024, following the legal action taken against the founders of Samourai Wallet.
