Bitcoin mining operation that is connected to the general power grid. On-grid miners plug into the conventional power grid, also used by residential consumers, to power their ASICs. This method provides a stable energy source, but is often more expensive than off-grid mining, because of more limited negotiating margins.

Sometimes, on-grid mining farms take part in demand response programs. They adjust their energy consumption according to fluctuations in electricity production and consumption on the grid, in order to maintain balance and avoid waste or blackouts.
