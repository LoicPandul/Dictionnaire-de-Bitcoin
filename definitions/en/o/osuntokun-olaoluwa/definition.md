Developer, cofounder and chief technology officer of Lightning Labs, lead author of lnd, coauthor of BIP-0157 and BIP-0158, and, since 2024, one of the BIP editors. He signs as roasbeef.

His first commit in the lnd repository dates from October 27, 2015. As of October 26, 2026, he remains the author of 6,099 of the 21,603 commits on the main branch. Lightning Labs, founded with Elizabeth Stark, released lnd 0.4-beta on March 15, 2018, the first version that runs on the Bitcoin mainnet.

On June 1, 2017, he proposed on bitcoin-dev, with Alex Akselrod, client-side filtering for light clients, as an alternative to the Bloom filters of BIP-0037. Full nodes serve a compact filter of each block, the client tests it locally and downloads the block only if an element concerns it. The filter construction became BIP-0158, based on Golomb-Rice coding, and the protocol became BIP-0157, which Jim Posen co-signs. Both BIPs were assigned on May 24, 2017, and their status is “Deployed.”

In April 2018, he announced with Christian Decker and Rusty Russell eltoo, a state replacement mechanism for second-layer protocols, based on state numbers that the chain enforces. eltoo requires `SIGHASH_NOINPUT`, which became `SIGHASH_ANYPREVOUT`.

In April 2024, he joined Bryan Bishop, Murch, Ruben Somsen, and Jon Atack among the BIP editors. BIP-0003 still lists him.
