Bitcoin mining pool launched in November 2023 by Luke Dashjr, a longtime developer of Bitcoin Core and Bitcoin Knots. Ocean stands out for its focus on decentralizing mining: miners can select or build their own block templates, which reduces dependence on centralized pool operators. The pool uses the TIDES payout system (Transparent Index of Distinct Extended Shares), which rewards miners directly through the outputs of coinbase transactions. The project continues Eligius, the mining pool Luke Dashjr launched in 2011.
