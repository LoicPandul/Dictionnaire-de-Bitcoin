Checks whether the third item from the top of the stack (`x`) lies within the range defined by the two items above it: `min`, the second from the top, and `max`, the top of the stack. In other words, `OP_WITHIN` checks whether `x` is greater than or equal to `min` and less than `max`. It removes these three items, then, if this condition is true, it pushes `1` (true) onto the stack, otherwise it pushes `0` (false).
