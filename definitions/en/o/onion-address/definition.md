Special address used to access an onion service on the Tor network. It ends with the suffix `.onion` and is not resolved by conventional DNS: only a client connected to the Tor network can reach it. A v3 onion address (the current format) is a string of 56 alphanumeric characters derived from the service's `Ed25519` public key, which lets the client cryptographically verify that it is communicating with the right server.

In the Bitcoin ecosystem, onion addresses allow Bitcoin and Lightning nodes to expose their services without revealing their real IP address. A node configured with a `.onion` address remains reachable by its peers on the P2P network, while concealing its geographic location and the identity of its operator. This strengthens censorship resistance and privacy, at the cost of slightly higher latency than the clearnet.
