Imaginary pill, inspired by the film *Matrix*, that awakens awareness of Bitcoin. In the 1999 film, the character Morpheus offers Neo a choice between a blue pill, which would send him back to his comfortable illusion, and a red pill, which would show him reality. The Bitcoin community took up the image, replacing red with orange, the color of the Bitcoin logo.

Taking the orange pill means understanding what Bitcoin changes about money, and no longer seeing money the same way. Bringing someone to this point of view is called "orange-pilling" that person.

*Also used as a verb: to orange-pill someone is to enlist them in Bitcoin.*
