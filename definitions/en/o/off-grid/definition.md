Bitcoin mining operation that runs autonomously, without being connected to the main power grid. Off-grid miners generally use alternative energy sources such as solar, wind, or hydroelectric power, or generators running on fossil fuels (for example, gas that would otherwise be flared). This model is often favored to reduce energy costs, because it relies in part on energy that, without mining, would be wasted. Off-grid miners have the advantage of being able to set up in remote locations, in order to consume this energy that is too far from population centers.

Off-grid mining often has environmental benefits, because it can, in some cases, help reduce greenhouse gas emissions by limiting flaring. Moreover, it makes some renewable energy production projects economically viable despite their natural intermittency.
