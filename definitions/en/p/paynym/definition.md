Unique identifier linked to a Bitcoin wallet that implements this option. Paynyms were available on Samourai Wallet, whose servers and domain were seized in April 2024, and they are supported by Sparrow Wallet. Originally, these identifiers are generated from the wallet's payment code, in accordance with BIP-0047. They thus offer the ability to connect with another user to generate fresh, unique receiving addresses, without requiring direct interaction. The use of Paynyms was later extended to support various other features of the Samourai ecosystem. They are notably used to enable encrypted exchanges over the Soroban communication protocol, in order to carry out collaborative transactions easily. Paynyms can also be used for authentication on systems compatible with the AUTH47 protocol. Each Paynym is characterized by a unique identifier and is represented by a robot illustration.
