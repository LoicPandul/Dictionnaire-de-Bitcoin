Mobile app for peer-to-peer (P2P) bitcoin trading, founded in Switzerland in 2022. Peach Bitcoin, often simply called "Peach", lets users buy and sell bitcoin directly with each other without going through a centralized exchange.

The app uses an escrow system to secure trades and supports many payment methods in Europe, such as SEPA transfers, Revolut, PayPal, or cash payments at in-person meetups.
