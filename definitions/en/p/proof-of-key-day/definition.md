Annual initiative observed every January 3 (the anniversary of the genesis block) that encourages bitcoin holders to all withdraw their assets from exchanges at the same time and move them to self-custody solutions. The goal is to test the solvency of exchanges, by forcing them to prove that they actually hold their users' bitcoin and are not practicing fractional reserve banking. Ultimately, the best way to protect against exchange bankruptcies remains keeping one's bitcoin in self-custody all year round.
