Event celebrated every May 22 by the Bitcoin community that commemorates the first purchase of a physical good with bitcoin. In 2010, Laszlo Hanyecz, a Bitcoin developer, [proposed on the BitcoinTalk forum](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=137.msg1141#msg1141) to buy two large pizzas for 10,000 BTC, then worth about $40. On May 22, he confirmed that the offer had been accepted by a 19-year-old Californian student, Jeremy Sturdivant, known by the pseudonym Jercos. Jercos ordered the pizzas from Papa John's and had them delivered to Laszlo in Florida. This day marks an important moment for Bitcoin, since it demonstrates its potential as a medium of exchange. Every year, the community celebrates the event by eating pizzas paid for in bitcoin.
