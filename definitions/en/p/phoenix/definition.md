Self-custodial mobile wallet software designed to simplify transactions on the Lightning protocol and make them more accessible. It lets users manage their funds directly from their mobile devices without connecting to a separate Lightning node. In fact, Phoenix works as a true standalone Lightning node on the phone (the lightning-kmp implementation). It supports Bitcoin and Lightning transactions, and offers features that ease node management, such as automatic channel management with the ACINQ node. The user thus keeps control of their keys and bitcoin (self-custody) without having to manage their own channels. Phoenix is a project developed and maintained by the French company ACINQ.
