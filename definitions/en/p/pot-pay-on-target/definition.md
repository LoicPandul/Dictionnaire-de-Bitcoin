Method of calculating miner payouts in mining pools. POT is a payout system that varies with the difficulty of the work sent to the pool rather than that of the work assigned by the pool. In this approach, the reward for each share submitted by a miner is based on the difficulty of that specific share. This means that harder shares are rewarded better than easier ones. It is a variant of PPS, but by adjusting the reward according to the actual difficulty of the work done, POT involves much more variance for the miner.
