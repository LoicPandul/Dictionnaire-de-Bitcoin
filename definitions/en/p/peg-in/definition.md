Mechanism by which the value of bitcoin is transferred from the main blockchain to a sidechain. The process consists of locking BTC on the main chain, either by sending them to a special address managed by a trusted third party or a group, or to a script that makes them temporarily unspendable, and then issuing an equivalent amount of tokens on the sidechain. This lock ensures that the bitcoin cannot be spent on both chains at the same time. Once the peg-in is done, the user can use the features specific to the sidechain. The peg-in is the first step of the two-way peg that links the main chain to the sidechain.

An individual does not generally need a peg-in, since atomic swap systems exist that make it possible to exchange on-chain bitcoin for bitcoin on Liquid (or on another sidechain, where applicable).
