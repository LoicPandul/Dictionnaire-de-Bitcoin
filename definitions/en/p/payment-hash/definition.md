Hash of the preimage used to secure a payment on the Lightning Network. When a recipient creates an invoice, it generates a random secret (the preimage), computes its hash, and embeds it in the invoice. The sender then builds a chain of hashed time-locked contracts (HTLCs) locked by this hash along the payment route. The payment is finalized only when the recipient reveals the matching preimage, which lets each intermediate node unlock its funds in turn.
