Acronym for *Pseudorandom Number Generator*. Category of algorithms used to generate sequences of numbers that approximate randomness, starting from an initial state called a seed. In cryptography, a PRNG is used to produce keys, initialization vectors, and other elements that need randomness. A good PRNG must have properties such as uniform outputs, unpredictability, and resistance to predictive attacks. Unlike true random number generators, PRNGs are deterministic and reproducible. On Bitcoin, PRNGs can be used in wallet management software or hardware wallets to generate the mnemonic phrase that underlies hierarchical deterministic wallets.
