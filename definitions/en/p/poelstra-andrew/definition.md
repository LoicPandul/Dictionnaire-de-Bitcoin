Cryptography researcher, director of research at Blockstream in 2023, who has worked on sidechains, scriptless scripts, zero-knowledge proofs, Schnorr multisignatures, Miniscript, and seed backup.

On October 22, 2014, he was one of the nine authors of the pegged sidechains white paper, with Adam Back, Matt Corallo, Luke Dashjr, Mark Friedenbach, Gregory Maxwell, Andrew Miller, Jorge Timón, and Pieter Wuille. In October 2017, he put online the reference repository on scriptless scripts, an approach that avoids executing an explicit contract in a script and whose basic building blocks are adaptor signatures and multisignatures. In November 2017, he co-signed with Benedikt Bünz, Jonathan Bootle, Dan Boneh, Pieter Wuille, and Gregory Maxwell the paper on Bulletproofs, short proofs for Confidential Transactions, published at the IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy in 2018. In January 2018, he filed with Gregory Maxwell, Yannick Seurin, and Pieter Wuille the MuSig paper, a Schnorr multisignature scheme.

At Blockstream Research, he designed and implemented Miniscript with Pieter Wuille and Sanket Kanjalkar. BIP-0093, dated February 2023, describes codex32, a backup format for BIP-0032 seeds split into shares with Shamir's secret sharing. Poelstra signs it with Leon Olsson Curr and Pearlwort Sneed. BIP-0379, dated October 2023, specifies Miniscript; he is a coauthor with Pieter Wuille, Sanket Kanjalkar, Antoine Poinsot, and Ava Chow.
