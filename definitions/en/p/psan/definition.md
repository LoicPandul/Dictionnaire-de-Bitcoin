Acronym for *Prestataire de Services sur Actifs Numériques* (digital asset service provider). French regulatory status introduced by the PACTE Law of May 22, 2019, defining the legal framework for companies that offer cryptocurrency-related services in France. The services concerned include custody of digital assets, buying and selling against fiat currency, exchange between digital assets, and operation of trading platforms.

Since 2019, mandatory registration with the AMF (Autorité des Marchés Financiers) has been required to carry out these activities in France. An optional, more demanding authorization can also be obtained. Registration imposes obligations on anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (AML/CFT) and on identity verification (KYC).

With the entry into force of the European MiCA regulation (Markets in Crypto-Assets), the PSAN status is replaced by the harmonized European framework for crypto-asset service providers (CASPs, called PSCA in French, for *Prestataire de Services sur Crypto-Actifs*), applicable in all member states of the European Union. In France, PSANs already active before MiCA could continue without CASP authorization until July 1, 2026, according to the AMF.
