Service developed by Lightning Labs. It is a kind of marketplace for liquidity in Lightning channels. Pool connects users who need access to liquidity on LN with those who have capital to deploy on the network. Participants can earn satoshis by opening new channels for those who want to receive funds on Lightning for a set period. Pool also makes it possible to lease a channel to instantly accept Bitcoin payments. Lightning Labs launched Pool on November 2, 2020, as an alpha release on mainnet. The client software was still being released in 2026 (version 0.7.0-beta, in May 2026).
