Communication and data distribution model in which participants, often called nodes or peers, share their resources (such as files, processing power, bandwidth, assets...) directly with one another, without needing a centralized intermediary. In a P2P system, each participant acts simultaneously as a client (consumer of resources) and as a server (provider of resources).

The Bitcoin system operates on a P2P model, in which nodes are responsible for validating transactions and storing the blockchain. This means that, unlike traditional banking systems that depend on centralized entities, Bitcoin operates on a distributed structure in which no single entity holds control. The nodes of the Bitcoin network communicate with one another to propagate transactions and blocks, and to reach consensus on the state of the ledger.
