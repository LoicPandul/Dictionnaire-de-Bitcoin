Reverse mechanism of the peg-in, by which tokens circulating on a sidechain are destroyed to release the corresponding bitcoin on the main blockchain. The user starts the process by sending their tokens to a burn address on the sidechain, which triggers the unlocking of the BTC previously locked on the main chain. The peg-out is the second step of the two-way peg and lets the user return to the Bitcoin blockchain at any time. Depending on the implementation, the process can be managed by a federation of signers, as on Liquid, or handled automatically through cryptographic proofs.
