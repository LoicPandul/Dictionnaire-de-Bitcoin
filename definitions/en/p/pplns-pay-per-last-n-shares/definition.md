Method of calculating miner payouts in mining pools. PPLNS rewards miners according to their contribution in shares over a given period. In PPLNS, payments are made only when the pool finds a block and are based on the number of shares submitted by the miner relative to the total shares collected during the observed period. This method favors steady, long-term active miners, because it discourages pool hopping (frequently switching pools). The payout varies with the probability of finding a block, which can reduce the consistency of the miner's income (more variance than piece-rate payment).
