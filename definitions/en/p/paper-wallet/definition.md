Offline storage method that consists of printing or writing down, on paper (or possibly another physical medium) only, a pair of cryptographic keys giving access to bitcoin. Paper wallets are considered a cold storage method, because they are not connected to the internet, provided that the keys were never generated or printed by a device connected to the internet. Their security then rests on physical protection of the paper against loss, destruction, and theft. They are often considered obsolete today, because they offer a weaker security model than modern solutions. Moreover, their use often involves reusing the same public key, which is very problematic for the user's privacy.
