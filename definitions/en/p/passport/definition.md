Hardware wallet made by the American company Foundation Devices, designed exclusively for Bitcoin. The Passport is a fully air-gapped device that communicates with wallet software through a camera and QR codes, with no wired connection for signing transactions. It includes a secure element, and its firmware is open source. The device has a color screen and a directional pad, and is powered by a removable lithium-ion battery in the Nokia BL-5C format. The Passport works with the companion app Envoy, as well as with third-party software such as Sparrow Wallet.
