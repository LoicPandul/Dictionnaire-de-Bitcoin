Indicator of the network's computing power, measured in hashes per second (H/s). It shows the capacity of miners to perform hashing operations as part of proof of work. A high hashrate means greater security for Bitcoin's economic history and greater resistance to attacks, because a large amount of computing power would be needed to compromise the network.

The hashrate also indicates the competition between miners: the higher the hashrate, the higher the mining difficulty, which influences the distribution of rewards, and therefore miners' profitability. It is thus a key indicator of the health and security of the Bitcoin system. In the same way that the hashrate measures the overall computing power of the Bitcoin network, it can also measure the computing power of a single machine, a mining farm, or a mining pool.
