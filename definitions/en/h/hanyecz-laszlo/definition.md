American developer from the early Bitcoin community, known for paying 10,000 bitcoin for two pizzas in May 2010 and for being a pioneer of graphics card mining.

In May 2010, he published on the BitcoinTalk forum versions of the software for macOS, then, on May 10, a prototype for generating bitcoin with the graphics card (OpenCL). Until then, mining had been done with the processor; on his MacBook, GPU mining multiplied his computing power tenfold, according to his account reported by Bitcoin Magazine in 2019.

On May 18, 2010, he offered on the same forum 10,000 bitcoin to anyone who would have two large pizzas delivered to him. On May 22, he announced that the exchange had succeeded and thanked the user jercos, who ordered the pizzas for about 25 dollars. The 10,000 bitcoin were then worth about 41 dollars. Guinness World Records lists this purchase as the first commercial transaction in bitcoin. The community celebrates this date every May 22 as Pizza Day.

Hanyecz says he spent about 100,000 bitcoin in 2010, mostly by distributing them on the forum.
