Nonpartisan nonprofit organization founded in 2005 by Thor Halvorssen, dedicated to promoting human rights worldwide, with a particular focus on authoritarian societies. Since 2020, the foundation has run a Bitcoin Development Fund that finances open-source projects aimed at improving Bitcoin's privacy, decentralization, and accessibility. The HRF sees Bitcoin as a tool of financial emancipation for people living under authoritarian regimes, where access to the traditional banking system is restricted or monitored. The foundation also organizes educational events on Bitcoin and individual freedoms.
