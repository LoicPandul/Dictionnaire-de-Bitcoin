Peer-to-peer (P2P) bitcoin exchange platform that lets users buy and sell bitcoin directly with each other, without a custodial intermediary. Founded in 2016, Hodl Hodl differs from centralized exchanges in that it never holds users' funds. Trades are secured by a 2-of-3 multisig escrow system: the bitcoin is locked in a multisig address tied to three keys (buyer, seller, and Hodl Hodl), any two of which are enough to release the funds. This mechanism reduces counterparty risk while allowing trades without mandatory KYC procedures. Hodl Hodl also organizes the Baltic Honeybadger Conference, an annual event dedicated to Bitcoin.
