Acronym for *Human Readable Part*, a component of bech32 and bech32m receiving addresses (SegWit v0 and SegWit v1). The HRP is the portion of the address that is specifically formatted to be easily read and interpreted by humans. Let's take the example of a bech32 Bitcoin address:

```text
bc1qar0srrr7xfkvy5l643lydnw9re59gtzzwf5mdq
```

In this address, the initial `bc` is the HRP. This prefix lets a reader tell at a glance that the string of characters being presented is a Bitcoin address and nothing else.
