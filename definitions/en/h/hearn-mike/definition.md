Developer, author of the bitcoinj library, coauthor of BIP-0037 and BIP-0070, and initiator of the Bitcoin XT fork, who announced in January 2016 that he was leaving Bitcoin development.

The first commit of bitcoinj, a Java library for working with Bitcoin, dates from March 2011 and was authored by him. In 2012, he coauthored BIP-0037 with Matt Corallo. It defines the Bloom filters a light client uses to ask a peer only for the transactions that concern it (number assigned October 24, 2012). In 2013, he coauthored BIP-0070, the payment protocol, with Gavin Andresen (number assigned July 29, 2013). In 2014, he alone proposed BIP-0064, a `getutxo` command to query the UTXO set, later given Closed status.

On December 22, 2014, a commit he authored renamed the software “Bitcoin XT,” a set of patches on top of Bitcoin Core. Version 0.11A, tagged on August 6, 2015, and released on August 15, includes Andresen's BIP-0101, which increases the maximum block size. This fork is one episode of the Blocksize War.

On January 14, 2016, in a post titled “The resolution of the Bitcoin experiment,” he wrote that he was no longer taking part in Bitcoin development and had sold all his bitcoin. He argued there that Bitcoin had failed, and described a chain whose blocks are full, with the limit at 1 MB.
