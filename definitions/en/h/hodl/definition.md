Popular term in the Bitcoin community that refers to keeping one's bitcoin over the long term, despite market volatility, and not selling it. The term originated from a typo in [a 2013 post on the BitcoinTalk forum](https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=375643.msg4022997#msg4022997) by the user GameKyuubi, who seemed to be drunk and wrote "I AM HODLING" instead of "I AM HOLDING", meaning to keep. The word quickly became a meme and a slogan.
