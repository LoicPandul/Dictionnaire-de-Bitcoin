Said of a Bitcoin wallet that uses a single piece of information (the seed) to generate a multitude of public and private key pairs in a sequential, reproducible way. This way of managing keys is defined by the BIP-0032 standard. The main advantage of HD wallets is that they give users a multitude of different key pairs, notably to avoid address reuse, while letting them regenerate all of them from a single piece of information. The structure is called hierarchical because it makes it possible to organize multiple keys and addresses in a tree from a single seed. And it is deterministic in the sense that each seed always generates the same sequence of keys in any wallet that complies with this system.
