Proposal that specifies an alternative activation mechanism for the Taproot soft fork, based on BIP-0008 with the `LOT=true` parameter (mandatory activation). This BIP, proposed by Shinobius and Michael Folkson, was designed in parallel with the “Speedy Trial” deployed in Bitcoin Core, to guarantee that miners cannot block indefinitely the activation of a soft fork that has overwhelming community consensus. The signaling period started at block 681,408 with a 90% threshold, and provided for mandatory signaling before block 760,032 if lock-in had not been reached. In practice, Taproot was locked in through Bitcoin Core's Speedy Trial at block 687,285 in June 2021 and activated in November, which made BIP-0343 obsolete before its mandatory signaling phase was needed.
