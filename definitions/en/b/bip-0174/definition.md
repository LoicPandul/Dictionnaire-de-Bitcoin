Defines the PSBT (Partially Signed Bitcoin Transaction) format, a standard for exchanging unfinalized Bitcoin transactions between different actors such as software wallets, hardware wallets, or multisig coordinators. PSBT separates the steps of creating, updating, signing, and finalizing a transaction. The protocol defines six distinct roles: the Creator builds the base transaction, the Updater adds the necessary information (UTXOs, scripts), the Signer adds its signature, the Combiner merges the partial signatures, the Finalizer builds the final unlocking script, and the Extractor produces the transaction ready to be broadcast.

This format is used for multisigs, hardware wallets, coinjoins, and any other workflow where signing does not happen on the same device that builds the transaction.
