Proposal that defines MuSig2, a multisignature scheme in which *n* participants jointly produce a single Schnorr signature, indistinguishable on-chain from a regular individual signature. The protocol works in two rounds: an exchange of public nonces, then the generation of partial signatures that are combined into a final signature. Key aggregation is non-interactive: anyone can compute the aggregate public key from the individual public keys. MuSig2 thus enables an *n*-of-*n* multisig that appears on the blockchain as a single-key spend, which improves privacy and reduces transaction fees.
