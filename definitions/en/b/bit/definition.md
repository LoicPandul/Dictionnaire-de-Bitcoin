The word “bit” is a contraction of the terms “binary” and “digit”. In computing and cryptology, a bit is the fundamental unit of digital information and represents the smallest possible quantity of information. A bit can only take two distinct values: `0` or `1`. These values are also called binary states and can represent various things, such as the answers `yes` or `no`, `true` or `false`, and `on` or `off`. Bits are the foundation of digital systems and are used to store and transmit information in computers and networks.
