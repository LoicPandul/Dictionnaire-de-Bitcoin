Proposal "Compact Block Relay" aimed at reducing the bandwidth needed to transmit blocks on the Bitcoin network. Adopted in August 2016 in version 0.13.0 of Bitcoin Core, this protocol communicates block information compactly, based on the assumption that nodes already have a large share of the transactions of a recent block in their mempool. Rather than transmitting each transaction in full, which would be redundant, BIP-0152 proposes sending only short identifiers for the transactions already known to peers, along with a few selected transactions (notably the coinbase transaction and those the node is likely not to know). The node can then ask its peers for any missing transactions. Compact Block Relay thus reduces the amount of data exchanged during block propagation, which lowers bandwidth spikes and improves the overall efficiency of the network.
