Proposal by Alan Reiner, made in 2011, that aimed to standardize an exchange format for distributing and collecting signatures in multisignature transactions. Rather than introducing multisig itself, this BIP proposed a communication protocol called “Transaction Distribution Proposal” (TxDP), encoded in ASCII, which let the parties to a multisig transaction pass the proposed transaction to one another, collect the necessary signatures, and then broadcast the completed transaction. The format included all of the previous transactions it referenced, so that an offline signing device could verify the amounts without access to the blockchain. BIP-0010 was implemented in the Armory software but was later abandoned in favor of more recent formats. Its status is `Closed`.
