bLIP that defines LSPS0, the standardized transport layer for communication between Lightning service providers (LSPs) and their clients. The protocol reuses existing BOLT-08 peer-to-peer connections and carries JSON-RPC 2.0 messages over them using a unique message identifier (`37913`). The LSP acts as the JSON-RPC server and the client as the client, with named parameters and responses that may arrive out of order. LSPS0 also defines the common encoding schemas for amounts, fees, signatures, and addresses used by the higher-level LSPS specifications (LSPS1, LSPS2, and LSPS5). This architectural choice avoids introducing external dependencies such as HTTP or gRPC.
