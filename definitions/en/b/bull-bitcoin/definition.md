Canadian bitcoin exchange founded by Francis Pouliot. Bull Bitcoin stands out for its non-custodial model: unlike traditional exchanges, it does not take custody of its customers' bitcoin. Purchases are sent directly to the user's personal wallet, and sales are made from that same wallet.

The company follows a strict “Bitcoin only” philosophy and offers no other cryptocurrency. Bull Bitcoin supports payments over the Lightning Network for small transactions. The platform is particularly popular in Canada and has expanded to other markets. Its non-custodial model reduces counterparty risk for users, since the platform never holds the funds, which brings it closer to the principles of financial sovereignty promoted by Bitcoin.
