Software fork of Bitcoin Core that aims to test the integration of new Bitcoin Improvement Proposals in a controlled environment on test networks such as signet. It includes, for example, support for BIP-0118 (ANYPREVOUT) and BIP-0119 (CHECKTEMPLATEVERIFY). The goal of Bitcoin Inquisition is to experiment, in order to gain a better understanding of the applications, benefits, risks, and trade-offs of these changes. Bitcoin Inquisition is maintained by Anthony Towns.
