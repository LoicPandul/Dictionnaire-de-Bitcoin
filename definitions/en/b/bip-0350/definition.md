Specification that introduces Bech32m, a variant of the Bech32 address format (BIP-0173) that fixes a weakness in error detection for SegWit v1 and later addresses. The original Bech32 format had a flaw: inserting or deleting "q" characters right before a final "p" character was not always detected by the checksum. Bech32m fixes this problem by changing the final constant of the checksum. SegWit v0 addresses (starting with `bc1q`) keep the original Bech32 format, while v1 and later addresses (such as `bc1p` for Taproot) use Bech32m.
