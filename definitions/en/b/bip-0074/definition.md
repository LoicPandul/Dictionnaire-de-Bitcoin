Extension of the BIP-0070 payment protocol that allows zero-value `OP_RETURN` outputs to be included in payment requests. BIP-0074 aimed to allow the embedding of arbitrary data (metadata, order identifiers, proofs) in the transactions generated by the payment protocol, without destroying funds in those outputs. It was unanimously rejected by the community ("Unanimously Discourage for implementation"), its official status now being `Closed` (it was `Rejected` before 2025).
