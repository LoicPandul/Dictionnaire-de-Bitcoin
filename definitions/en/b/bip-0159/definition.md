Proposal defining the `NODE_NETWORK_LIMITED` service bit (bit 10) for pruned nodes on the Bitcoin network. This bit indicates that a node is able to serve at least the last 288 blocks of the blockchain (about 2 days), even without holding the full history. Before this BIP, pruned nodes could not signal their partial serving capability, which excluded them from block relay. BIP-0159 thus lets pruned nodes take an active part in the propagation of recent blocks, improving the network's decentralization without requiring storage of the entire chain.
