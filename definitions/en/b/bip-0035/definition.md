Proposal that lets a Bitcoin node expose information about its mempool, that is, the transactions waiting for confirmation. As a result, other parties can receive real-time data on unconfirmed transactions by sending a specific message to a node. Before BIP-0035, no message made it possible to ask a peer for the contents of its mempool. This improvement gives SPV wallets the ability to receive information about unconfirmed transactions, lets a miner avoid omitting high-fee transactions after a restart, and makes it easier for external services to analyze mempool data.
