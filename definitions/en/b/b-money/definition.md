Decentralized cryptocurrency concept proposed by Wei Dai in 1998. This system imagined a network in which participants would be identified only by public keys, and each transaction would be signed by the sender. B-money was built on an account model, unlike Bitcoin, which uses a UTXO model. It allowed money creation through a kind of proof of work tied to a basket of goods. It was therefore a precursor of today's stablecoin principle. The concept was never implemented.
