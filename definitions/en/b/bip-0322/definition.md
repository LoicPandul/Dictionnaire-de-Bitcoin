Proposal for a new standard, replacing BIP-0137, to sign messages with Bitcoin private keys and their associated addresses, to prove ownership of an address. These signatures are useful for various applications such as proof of funds, auditing, and other uses that require authenticating an address through its private key. Compared with BIP-0137, BIP-0322 extends the message signing standard beyond conventional addresses, using a script-based approach. It lets wallet software sign a message for any script it could unlock to spend bitcoin. To do so, the method signs a text by producing a signature for a virtual Bitcoin transaction. For traditional P2PKH addresses, BIP-0322 remains compatible with the legacy signature format.
