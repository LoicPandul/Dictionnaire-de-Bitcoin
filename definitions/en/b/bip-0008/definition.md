Soft fork activation method developed after the debates over SegWit, which used BIP-0009 for its activation, and which natively incorporates an automatic User-Activated Soft Fork (UASF) mechanism. Like BIP-0009, BIP-0008 relies on miner signaling, but it adds the `LOT` parameter (Lock-in On Timeout). If `LOT` is set to `true` and the signaling period expires without reaching the required threshold, a UASF is automatically triggered, forcing the soft fork to activate. This approach forces miners to cooperate or risk a UASF imposed by users. In addition, unlike BIP-0009, BIP-0008 defines the signaling period in terms of block height, which rules out potential manipulation by miners through hashrate. BIP-0008 also lets the voting threshold be set to a variable value and introduces a parameter for a minimum activation block height, giving miners time to prepare and signal their agreement in advance without necessarily being ready. When a soft fork is activated through BIP-0008 with `LOT=true`, the method is very aggressive toward miners, who are immediately put under pressure by a possible UASF. In effect, it leaves them only two choices:
* Cooperate, and thereby ease the activation process;
* Not cooperate, in which case users automatically run a UASF to impose the soft fork.
