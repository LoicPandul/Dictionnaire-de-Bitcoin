Proposal that defines the PSBT fields needed to send Silent Payments (BIP-0352). This BIP includes each signer's ECDH shares as well as the DLEQ proofs (BIP-0374) attesting that the computations are correct. BIP-0375 allows several signers in a multisig context to collaborate on computing the output addresses of Silent Payments by exchanging PSBT files, without each participant having to reveal their private key to the others.
