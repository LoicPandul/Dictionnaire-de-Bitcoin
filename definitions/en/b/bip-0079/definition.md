Proposal for a "pay-to-endpoint" (P2EP) protocol, also known as Bustapay. It predates BIP-0078. The receiver added one of its inputs to the payer's transaction, thereby breaking the common-input-ownership heuristic of chain analysis, which assumes that all inputs belong to the same owner. BIP-0079 was superseded by Payjoin (BIP-0078), which formalized and improved this collaborative transaction approach.
