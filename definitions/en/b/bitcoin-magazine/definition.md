Publication founded in 2012 by Mihai Alisie and Vitalik Buterin (before the latter created Ethereum), dedicated to Bitcoin news and analysis. Bitcoin Magazine is one of the oldest media outlets specializing in the Bitcoin ecosystem. It publishes articles, technical analyses, interviews, and educational content covering every aspect of Bitcoin: protocol, economics, regulation, mining, and development. The magazine exists as a website, a print publication, and video content. Bitcoin Magazine also organizes the "Bitcoin Conference", one of the largest annual events in the Bitcoin community, held in the United States and bringing together developers, entrepreneurs, investors, and enthusiasts from around the world.
