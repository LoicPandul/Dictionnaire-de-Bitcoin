Proposal presented by Gavin Andresen in 2015 that aimed to raise the maximum block size from 1 MB to 8 MB and to double this limit every two years until it reached a little over 8 GB in 2036. The proposal was included in the Bitcoin XT implementation in August 2015, which thereby became a de facto fork of Bitcoin, but it did not gain a following. The proposal was therefore never adopted. Some consider the implementation of BIP-0101 in Bitcoin XT to be the *casus belli* of the Blocksize War.
