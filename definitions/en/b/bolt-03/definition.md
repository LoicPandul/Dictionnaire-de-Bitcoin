Specification that defines the exact formats of the Bitcoin transactions and scripts used in Lightning channels. It details the structure of the funding transaction (a 2-of-2 multisig P2WSH output), the commitment transactions that represent the current state of the channel, with their `to_local` and `to_remote` outputs, and the HTLC transactions. BOLT-03 also specifies fee calculation, dust limits, key derivation procedures, and the expected transaction weights for correct fee estimation.
