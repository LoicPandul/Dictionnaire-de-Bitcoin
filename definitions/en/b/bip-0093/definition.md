Informational BIP suggesting a standard to back up and restore the seed of a hierarchical deterministic wallet (as defined in BIP-0032) using Shamir's Secret Sharing. The protocol defines the “codex32” format, inspired by the bech32 format, which introduces a structured string made of a prefix, a threshold parameter, an identifier, a share index, a payload, and a BCH checksum. The method splits the seed into up to 31 shares, of which a defined threshold (between 2 and 9) is required to fully recover the seed.
