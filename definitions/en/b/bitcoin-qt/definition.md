Bitcoin client with a graphical interface, released in November 2011. It follows in the line of Satoshi's own client. In 2014, Bitcoin QT was renamed "Bitcoin Core". Today it is the reference implementation of the Bitcoin protocol. It has shipped with `bitcoind` since version 0.5. Even today, Bitcoin Core's graphical interface is still called `bitcoin-qt`, in reference to the software's origins.

*"QT" comes from the name of the library used for the graphical interface. The name is sometimes read as a pun on the sound of the word "cute".*
