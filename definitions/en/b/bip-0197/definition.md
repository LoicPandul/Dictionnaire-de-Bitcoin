Proposal for HTLCCs (Hashed Time-Locked Collateral Contracts), an extension of HTLCs (Hashed Time-Locked Contracts) that adds a collateral mechanism. BIP-0197 is designed to create debt agreements between two parties (a borrower and a lender), where bitcoin collateral is locked in a P2SH to secure a loan whose principal is denominated on another blockchain. In case of non-repayment, the collateral can be liquidated through an atomic swap or seized under predefined conditions.
