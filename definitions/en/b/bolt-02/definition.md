Specification that defines the peer-to-peer protocol for managing Lightning channels. It covers the entire life cycle of a channel: establishment (in the classic version with a single funder, and in version 2 with dual funding through interactive transaction construction), normal operation (adding and resolving HTLCs, updating commitment transactions), and cooperative close. BOLT-02 also specifies the fee bumping mechanisms using RBF, channel quiescence for protocol updates, splicing (replacing the funding transaction with a new one, a section added to the specification in March 2026), and the message retransmission protocol when peers reconnect.
