Introduced a change in how timelock operations based on `nLockTime` and `OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY` are computed. It specifies that, to evaluate the validity of these timelocks, they must now be compared with the Median Time Past (MTP), that is, the median of the timestamps of the last 11 blocks. Previously, only the timestamp of the current block was used. This method makes the system more predictable and prevents miners from manipulating the time reference. BIP-0113 was introduced through a soft fork on July 4, 2016, together with BIP-0068 and BIP-0112, and was activated for the first time using the BIP-0009 method.
