Proposal by Luke Dashjr titled “hashScriptCheck” that aimed to replace the `scriptSig` and `scriptPubKey` fields of transactions with three new elements: `dataSig` (pure data pushed onto the stack), `scriptCheck` (the validation script), and `hashScriptCheck` (the hash of the expected script, placed in the output). Unlike BIP-0017 by the same author, which proposed a new `OP_CHECKHASHVERIFY` opcode, BIP-0018 took a more radical approach by redefining the very structure of transaction inputs and outputs. At the protocol level, the result was identical to BIP-0016 (P2SH), but BIP-0018 formalized the explicit deprecation of `scriptSig` and `scriptPubKey`.
