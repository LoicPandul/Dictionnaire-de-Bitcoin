bLIP that specifies hosted channels, Lightning payment channels based on the client's trust in a host, with no on-chain anchoring. Unlike regular Lightning channels, which lock bitcoin in a funding transaction, hosted channels work without a UTXO or any interaction with the blockchain. The channel state is signed by both parties through encrypted messages exchanged over the Lightning protocol, which prevents the host from unilaterally falsifying balances. The advantages include immediate receiving capacity and no on-chain fees, but the client bears a counterparty risk because it cannot force-close the channel on the blockchain. This model sits between a regular custodial wallet and a regular Lightning channel.
