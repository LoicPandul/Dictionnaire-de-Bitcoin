Non-custodial mobile Lightning Network wallet for sending and receiving bitcoin payments almost instantly. The app embedded a Lightning node directly on the user's device, giving them full control of their funds without relying on a trusted third party. In 2026, Breez released a backup recovery tool (Breez Mobile Recovery), intended to keep funds accessible after the app's removal from the app stores.

The app stood out for its simplified interface, which hid the technical complexity of the Lightning Network, notably the management of payment channels and liquidity. It also included point-of-sale features, letting small merchants accept Lightning payments directly from their phone. It supported the LNURL and Lightning Address protocols to simplify receiving payments. The company also offers the Breez SDK, an open-source development kit that lets other apps integrate Lightning payments with no node to manage. In 2026, this SDK builds on Spark, a payment layer built on Bitcoin.
