Open-source project for a miniature Bitcoin miner built around an ASIC chip. The Bitaxe is a small mining device designed to be accessible, inexpensive, and fully transparent in its design, with hardware schematics and firmware published as open source. It uses mining ASIC chips (taken from consumer machines such as Bitmain's Antminer series) soldered onto a compact, self-contained circuit board powered by a simple USB-C cable. The Bitaxe is mainly used for educational purposes or for “lottery mining” (solo mining in the hope of finding a block by chance), since its computing power remains very low compared with large industrial mining farms.
