Bitcoin mining pool operated by the Czech company Braiins. Created in 2010 by Marek Palatinus (“Slush”), it is the very first Bitcoin mining pool, and it mined its first block on December 16, 2010. Initially known as “Bitcoin.cz Mining Pool”, then “Slush Pool” from 2016, it was renamed “Braiins Pool” in 2022. Braiins also designed, with Matt Corallo, the Stratum V2 protocol, a major update to the communication protocol between miners and pools, which improves security, reduces bandwidth, and lets miners select the transactions to include in blocks themselves. The company also develops Braiins OS, open-source firmware for ASIC machines.
