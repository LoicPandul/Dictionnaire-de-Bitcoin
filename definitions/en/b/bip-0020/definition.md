First version of the `bitcoin:` URI scheme, meant to make payments easier through clickable links. BIP-0020 notably proposed an amount system with exponents (for example `50X8` for $50 \times 10^8$ satoshis). It was replaced by BIP-0021, written by Nils Schneider and Matt Corallo on the basis of the work of Luke Dashjr, which removed these alternative amounts and extended the specification with additional parameters.
