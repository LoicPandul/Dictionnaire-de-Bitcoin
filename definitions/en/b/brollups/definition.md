Native rollup concept on Bitcoin proposed by Burak in June 2024. Unlike optimistic or zero-knowledge proof rollups, Brollups form a category of their own. They build on the VTXO (Virtual UTXO) concept from the Ark protocol and use VTXOs as the peg mechanism. The principle is to exchange VTXOs for calldata, accompanied by new VTXOs, through coinswaps. Brollups are run by an operator or a quorum of operators who provide liquidity to the protocol and advance the rollup state by chaining Bitcoin transactions at regular intervals. The Bitcoin blockchain serves as the data availability layer, while transactions are executed on a dedicated virtual machine called the Bitcoin Virtual Machine. The peg is executable on-chain and can be redeemed unilaterally at any time. Brollups require no change to the Bitcoin protocol and are not associated with any token.
