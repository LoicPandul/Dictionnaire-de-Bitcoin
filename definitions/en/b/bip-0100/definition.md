Proposal by Jeff Garzik during the Blocksize War that would let miners vote to dynamically adjust the maximum block size, starting from 1 MB and by at most 5% in each period of 2,016 blocks. The mechanism relied on a vote in each block, in which miners expressed their preference for an increase or a decrease of the limit. This BIP-0100 was rejected, as the community ultimately opted for SegWit rather than a direct increase in block size.
