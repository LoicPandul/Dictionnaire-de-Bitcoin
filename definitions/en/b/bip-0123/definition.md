Establishes a new standardized process for classifying Bitcoin Improvement Proposals. BIPs must from now on be classified into 4 layers:
* `Consensus`: covers proposals that require a consensus change and affect compatibility between earlier and future versions of the Bitcoin protocol. These are soft forks and hard forks;
* `Peer Services`: covers changes to the services and communication protocols between nodes of the network, without affecting consensus;
* `API/RPC`: covers proposals to change the APIs and RPCs used to interact with Bitcoin nodes;
* `Applications`: covers proposed improvements for applications that run on top of the Bitcoin network, typically standards related to wallet software.
