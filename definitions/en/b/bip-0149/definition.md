Proposal by Shaolin Fry for a new deployment of SegWit (BIP-0141, BIP-0143, and BIP-0147) using the BIP-0008 activation method with `LOT=true`, in case the initial SegWit deployment through BIP-0009 failed to activate before November 15, 2017. Unlike the BIP-0009 method, where a signaling failure means activation is abandoned, BIP-0149 aimed to activate SegWit on July 4, 2018, whether or not miners had reached the 95% signaling threshold. During the eight-month period between November and July, nodes would have had the option to implement BIP-0149, in order to ensure SegWit's activation by the economic majority of the network if miner activation did not occur (UASF). Once the first difficulty adjustment after July 4, 2018 was reached, activation would have moved to `LOCKED_IN`, and SegWit would have been activated at the next adjustment cycle. Unlike BIP-0148, whose nodes rejected blocks not signaling SegWit from August 1, 2017, BIP-0149 suggested a more gradual and measured activation method, although it remained offensive, following the principles of BIP-0008. Whereas BIP-0148 foreshadowed a conflict with a blockchain split, BIP-0149 ruled out that possibility by accepting blocks that did not signal SegWit, barring a deliberate action by a miner (with no incentive). BIP-0149 was therefore a less confrontational SegWit activation mechanism than BIP-0148, favoring a more gradual and less risky adoption for the system. BIP-0149 was ultimately not deployed, as SegWit was activated through a MASF, notably under the impetus of BIP-0091.
