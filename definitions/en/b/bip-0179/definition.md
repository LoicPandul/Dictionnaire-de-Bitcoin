Informational document proposing to replace the term "address" with "invoice" (or "Bitcoin Invoice Address") to designate payment-receiving identifiers in Bitcoin. BIP-0179 starts from the observation that the word "address" suggests permanent information (like a postal or email address), whereas Bitcoin addresses are designed for single use. The term "invoice" is proposed to better reflect this ephemeral nature and the link to a specific transaction. The permitted variants are "Bitcoin Invoice Address," "Bitcoin Invoice," "Invoice Address," and "Invoice," with the suffix "Address" tolerated as a transitional measure.
