Proposal to fix two residual sources of signature malleability in Bitcoin transactions, after the adoption of strict DER encoding by BIP-0066. The first rule, “LOW_S,” requires the S value of ECDSA signatures to be in the lower half of the curve order, thereby eliminating the malleability inherent to ECDSA signatures. The second rule, “NULLFAIL,” requires that any signature failing verification through `OP_CHECKSIG` or `OP_CHECKMULTISIG` be an empty byte array, thereby preventing invalid signatures from being replaced with other invalid values. BIP-0146 has the status “closed,” because these fixes were partially integrated into the standardness rules and into SegWit (BIP-0141), which solved the malleability problem more completely.
