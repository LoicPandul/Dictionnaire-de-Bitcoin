Online service with no KYC for buying gift cards, phone top-ups, and subscriptions with bitcoin. Bitrefill is a gateway between the Bitcoin economy and the traditional economy: users can spend their bitcoin at hundreds of retailers (Amazon, Uber, Steam, etc.) without those retailers directly accepting bitcoin payments.

The service supports on-chain and Lightning Network payments, which allows near-instant transactions. Bitrefill is often used by bitcoiners who want to live on BTC as much as possible while still accessing the goods and services of traditional commerce.
