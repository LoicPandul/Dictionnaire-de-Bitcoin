First bLIP, which establishes the process for creating and managing bLIPs (Bitcoin Lightning Improvement Proposals). It defines the life cycle of a proposal: submission of a draft, review by the editors for technical soundness and formatting, then progression through the `Draft`, `Proposed`, and `Final`/`Active` statuses. bLIP-0001 spells out the distinction from BOLTs: bLIPs cover application-level features and non-universal extensions, while BOLTs remain reserved for protocol changes that require adoption by all implementations.
