Standardization of the signature format in transactions. This BIP was proposed in reaction to a divergence in how OpenSSL handled signature encoding on different systems. This heterogeneity posed a risk of splitting the blockchain. BIP-0066 unified the signature format for all transactions by using strict DER (Distinguished Encoding Rules) encoding. This change required a soft fork. For its activation, BIP-0066 used the same mechanism as BIP-0034, which raised the `nVersion` field to version 3 and rejected all blocks of version 2 or lower once the threshold of 95% of miners was reached. This threshold was crossed at block 363,725 on July 4, 2015.
