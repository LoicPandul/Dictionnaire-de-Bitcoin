Proposal for a new proof-of-work paradigm called "Optical Proof of Work" (oPoW), aimed at reducing the energy consumption of Bitcoin mining. BIP-0052 introduces "HeavyHash", a cryptographic construction that combines a Keccak (SHA-3) hash with a matrix-vector multiplication. This multiplication is designed to run optimally on photonic coprocessors, a new class of chips that use light instead of electrical currents to perform computations. The goal is to shift mining costs from operating expenses (OPEX), mainly electricity, to capital expenditure (CAPEX), that is, hardware. This would make mining profitable regardless of the local cost of electricity, favoring greater geographic decentralization. Although the BIP is classified as a hard fork, the authors suggest that oPoW could be implemented through a soft fork as a dual PoW (SHA-256 and oPoW in parallel). This proposal was marked `Closed` in 2026 for lack of progress in several years.
