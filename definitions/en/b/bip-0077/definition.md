Proposal for an asynchronous version of the Payjoin protocol (BIP-0078). BIP-0077 lets the receiver be offline when the payment is initiated. The payer publishes an encrypted PSBT on a relay server, which the receiver retrieves and co-signs later. This approach considerably improves the usability of the original Payjoin, which required both parties to be online at the same time to complete the collaborative transaction. The protocol keeps the same privacy benefits.
