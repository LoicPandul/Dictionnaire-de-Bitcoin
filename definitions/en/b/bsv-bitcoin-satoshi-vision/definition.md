Cryptocurrency system resulting from a hard fork of Bitcoin Cash (BCH), itself a fork of Bitcoin (BTC). The Bitcoin SV fork took place on November 15, 2018 at block 556,766, because of disagreements within the Bitcoin Cash community, notably over the block size and the supposed vision of Satoshi Nakamoto, the creator of Bitcoin. Two camps clashed:
* The supporters of the "Bitcoin Cash ABC" fork, which became Bitcoin Cash (BCH). This group was backed notably by the well-known entrepreneur Roger Ver;
* The promoters of the "Bitcoin Cash Satoshi Vision" fork, which led to the creation of Bitcoin Satoshi Vision (BSV). This camp was backed, among others, by Craig Wright.

Bitcoin SV differs from Bitcoin Cash, and even more from Bitcoin, in its considerably higher block size limit. This feature is meant to align with what its supporters consider to be Satoshi Nakamoto's original vision for Bitcoin.
