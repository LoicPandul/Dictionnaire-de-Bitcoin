bLIP that defines LSPS5, a webhook registration protocol that lets Lightning service providers (LSPs) notify mobile clients when an event needs their attention. Mobile applications, suspended by the operating system when they are not active, lose their network connections and cannot receive payments or handle expiring HTLCs. With LSPS5, the client registers a webhook URL with the LSP through three APIs: `lsps5.set_webhook`, `lsps5.list_webhooks`, and `lsps5.remove_webhook`. The LSP contacts this webhook when an incoming payment arrives, when an HTLC is about to expire, when liquidity changes, or when an onion message must be delivered. The notifications are cryptographically signed by the identity key of the LSP node.
