Sidechain consensus technique that lets Bitcoin miners work simultaneously on the main chain and on one or more sidechains, without having to perform more computing work. Unlike standard merged mining, this method does not require setting up a new node for each sidechain being mined. In Blind Merged Mining (BMM), each sidechain is run by independent node operators, who are responsible for creating blocks and collecting the associated rewards on the sidechain. In return, these operators must buy proofs of work from miners of the main blockchain to validate their blocks on the sidechain. As a result, Bitcoin miners receive their merged mining rewards for the sidechains in BTC, directly on the main chain. This method, developed by Paul Sztorc for drivechains, requires the implementation of BIP-0301 to be operational on the Bitcoin network.
