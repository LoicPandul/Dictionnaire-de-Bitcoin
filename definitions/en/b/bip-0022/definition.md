BIP proposed in 2012 by Luke Dashjr that introduces a standardized JSON-RPC method for external mining interfaces, called `getblocktemplate`. As mining difficulty increased, external software specialized in producing proofs of work became widespread. This BIP proposes a common communication standard for the block template between full nodes and mining software. The method involves sending the entire block structure, rather than just the header, so that the miner can customize it.
