Alternative name for the "score-based method" of calculating miner payouts in mining pools. It is a proportional payout method, but weighted by the moment at which each share is submitted. BPM values shares according to the time elapsed since the start of the mining cycle: the later in the cycle a share is submitted, the higher its value. The method gives miners an incentive to stay, since each time a miner stops mining, their score stalls while the scores of the others rise faster and faster.

"Bitcoin Pooled Mining" was also the name of Slush's mining pool in 2010, which is the origin of the payout calculation method of the same name.
