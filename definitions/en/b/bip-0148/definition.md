Proposal introduced in March 2017 by a developer under the pseudonym Shaolin Fry. The goal of BIP-0148 was to force the activation of the SegWit upgrade on the Bitcoin protocol, as miner signaling for this soft fork stagnated under the BIP-0009 method. BIP-0148 suggested implementing a UASF (User-Activated Soft Fork) to force SegWit's activation by nodes: its rule was to apply from August 1 to November 15, 2017, if miners had not locked in SegWit before August 1, 2017. Had the BIP-0148 UASF been adopted, nodes on the Bitcoin network would have refused blocks that did not signal support for SegWit, thereby putting pressure on miners to adopt the upgrade. Although this historic BIP was ultimately not activated, it played a decisive role in the successful adoption of SegWit, by pushing miners to lock in the soft fork through BIP-0091. In the longer term, BIP-0148 set an important precedent, demonstrating the influence that users can exert through their full nodes on governance decisions of the Bitcoin protocol.
