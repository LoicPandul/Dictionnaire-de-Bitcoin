bLIP that proposes an experimental mechanism for signaling the endorsement of HTLCs to fight channel jamming attacks on the Lightning Network. Each HTLC carries a one-byte TLV field indicating whether it is endorsed (value 7) or not (value 0). Originating nodes mark as endorsed the payments they expect to succeed. Intermediate nodes propagate the positive signal if they are compatible, and otherwise forward the default value of zero. This mechanism is purely observational: the data collected is used to test reputation algorithms without affecting the actual routing of payments. The experiment is limited in time by an expiration date, after which nodes stop relaying the signal.
