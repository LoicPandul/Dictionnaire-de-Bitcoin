Specification that defines the encoding format of Lightning payment invoices (payment requests), designed to be compatible with QR codes. A BOLT-11 invoice is encoded in Bech32 and consists of three parts: a human-readable prefix indicating the network and an optional amount (with multipliers `m`, `u`, `n`, `p`), a data section containing a timestamp and tagged fields, and an ECDSA signature. The tagged fields carry the payment hash, the payment secret, the description, the expiry time, routing hints for private channels, and feature flags. BOLT-11 is gradually being complemented by BOLT-12, which brings reusable invoices and better privacy.
