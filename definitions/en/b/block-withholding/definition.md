Attack specific to pool mining, in which a pool participant finds a block with a valid proof of work but does not share it with the pool. The attacker submits partial proofs of work (shares) to keep up the appearance of active participation, but withholds the valid proof of work, depriving the pool of the rewards for that block. This tactic aims to reduce the pool's earnings without any direct benefit to the attacker, while hurting the pool's profitability.
