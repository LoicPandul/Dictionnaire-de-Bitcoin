Extension of BIP-0022 meant to encourage its adoption by mining pools. Proposed by Luke Dashjr, it extends the `getblocktemplate` standard of BIP-0022 to cover all the features of the Getwork protocol, the ancestor of Stratum, in order to ease the transition of pools from Getwork to `getblocktemplate`. The main goal of BIP-0023 is to send the complete block template to miners, letting them audit and possibly modify the block structure proposed by the pool. This aims to ease concerns about mining centralization by giving individual miners more control over the block creation process, and more transparency into it.
