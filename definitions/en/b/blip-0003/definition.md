bLIP that formalizes the specification of keysend, a spontaneous payment mechanism on the Lightning Network. With keysend, the sender generates a random 32-byte preimage, includes it in a TLV record (type `5482373484`) of the onion packet, and uses its SHA-256 hash as the payment hash. The recipient checks that the preimage matches the hash to accept the payment. Keysend is expected to be replaced eventually by more complete mechanisms such as BOLT-12 offers.
