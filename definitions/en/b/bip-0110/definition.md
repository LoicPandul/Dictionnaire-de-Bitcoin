Proposal for a temporary (one-year) soft fork that limits, at the consensus level, the size of arbitrary data that can be inscribed in Bitcoin transactions, in order to fight the use of the system as a data storage medium (notably the Ordinals inscriptions that appeared in 2022).

In practice, BIP-0110 invalidates `scriptPubKey` values longer than 34 bytes (except `OP_RETURN`, limited to 83 bytes), witness elements and `OP_PUSHDATA` payloads exceeding 256 bytes, spends of undefined witness versions, the Taproot annex, Taproot control blocks longer than 257 bytes (which limits the tree to 128 leaves), as well as the `OP_SUCCESS` opcodes and `OP_IF`/`OP_NOTIF` in Tapscript. UTXOs created before activation are exempt.

Deployment uses a modified BIP-0009 with the threshold lowered to 55%, a mandatory activation by block height, and an active duration of 52,416 blocks (about 1 year), after which the rules expire automatically. This BIP is one of the most controversial of the post-Ordinals era: its supporters see it as a defense of Bitcoin as money, while its detractors denounce an arbitrary restriction of block space. The BIP repository moved it to the `Closed` status in August 2026.
