Major revision of the BIP process, discussed from 2024 and activated in January 2026, replacing BIP-0002, which had been in force since 2016. BIP-0003 simplifies the workflow by reducing the previous nine statuses to four: `Draft`, `Complete`, `Deployed`, and `Closed`. It redefines the BIP types by replacing `Standards Track` with `Specification`, making clear that every BIP is an individual recommendation from its authors and not a standard imposed on anyone. The role of BIP editors is refocused on administrative and editorial tasks, with less discretionary judgment over whether proposals are accepted.

Among the novelties, BIP-0003 introduces semantic versioning with a mandatory `Changelog` once a BIP reaches `Complete` status, the option to appoint `Deputies` to assist authors, and the use of SPDX expressions for licenses. The Markdown format is now accepted in addition to MediaWiki. The comment system of BIP-0002, judged ineffective for lack of participation, is abolished. BIPs of type `Process` become living documents that can be amended indefinitely by rough consensus. BIP-0003 has `Deployed` status.
