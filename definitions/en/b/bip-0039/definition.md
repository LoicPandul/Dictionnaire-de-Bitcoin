Proposal that introduces a method for converting random entropy into a sequence of memorable, human-readable words, known as a mnemonic phrase. This phrase, usually made up of 12 or 24 words, is then used to derive the wallet's seed, and therefore to regenerate all of its private keys deterministically. Instead of having to memorize or store a complex cryptographic seed, users can thus back up their bitcoin with a phrase of a few words. BIP-0039 has thereby helped simplify the management of a Bitcoin wallet.
