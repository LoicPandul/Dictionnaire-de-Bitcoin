Specification that defines the behavior of Lightning nodes when a channel is closed on-chain. It covers three scenarios: cooperative close, unilateral close (publication of a commitment transaction), and close by revocation (an attempted fraud). BOLT-05 details the resolution of each type of output (local, remote, anchor, and HTLC outputs), the rules for monitoring the blockchain until all funds are irrevocably resolved (100 blocks deep), and the penalty transactions that make it possible to seize all of the channel's funds when an old, revoked state is published. The specification also describes the fee bumping mechanisms using anchor outputs.
