Document that defines the process for developing and implementing improvements to the Bitcoin protocol. Written by Amir Taaki in September 2011, it establishes a standard methodology for proposing and documenting potential changes. BIP proposals are sorted into three types:
* `Standards Track`: those that concern direct changes to the Bitcoin protocol and its interoperability;
* `Informational`: those that provide general information but do not directly affect the protocol;
* `Process`: those that introduce non-technical changes, such as procedures and guidelines.

This framework systematizes the development of Bitcoin, ensuring a coordinated and transparent approach to its evolution. The methodology of BIP-0001 was later replaced by that of BIP-0002.
