Hardware wallet developed by Block, Inc. (formerly Square), the company founded by Jack Dorsey. Launched in December 2023, Bitkey relies on a 2-of-3 multisig architecture that spreads the keys across the physical device, a mobile app, and an encrypted recovery server managed by Block. This design aims to simplify self-custody for non-technical users by removing the need to manage a mnemonic phrase themselves. The device has a compact form that includes a fingerprint sensor and communicates with the app over NFC.
