Proposes a standardized format for signing messages with Bitcoin private keys and their associated addresses, in order to prove ownership of an address. This BIP aims to resolve the ambiguity caused by the different types of Bitcoin addresses (P2PKH, P2SH-P2WPKH, P2WPKH, and so on) when signing a message. It introduces a method that explicitly distinguishes these address formats through the signatures. These signatures are useful for various applications such as proof of funds, auditing, and other uses that require authenticating an address through its private key. BIP-0322 has since introduced a new signature format that extends this standard and generalizes it to any type of script.
