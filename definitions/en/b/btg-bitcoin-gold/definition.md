Cryptocurrency system created from a hard fork of Bitcoin (BTC), which took place on October 24, 2017 at block 491,407. The fork was started by a group of developers and investors unhappy with the direction Bitcoin had taken, in particular the growing concentration of mining power in the hands of a few large farms using ASICs. Bitcoin Gold wanted to democratize the mining process by using a new proof-of-work algorithm, called Equihash, which is ASIC-resistant and therefore favors mining with graphics cards (GPUs). The goal was to make mining accessible to more people, in order to decentralize it and reduce the risks linked to centralization. BTG initially used the same mining algorithm as Zcash (ZEC), namely Equihash with the parameters (200,9). From block 536,200, in July 2018, it was changed to Equihash-BTG, a variant with the parameters (144,5), to strengthen resistance to ASICs.

*Note that the altcoin Bitcoin Gold (BTG) must not be confused with bit gold, Nick Szabo's concept.*
