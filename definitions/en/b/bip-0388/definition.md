Proposal that defines "wallet policies", a restricted subset of output script descriptors optimized for hardware wallets. Full descriptors can be arbitrarily complex and hard to display on the limited screens of signing devices. BIP-0388 simplifies the format by separating the structure of the descriptor (the "template") from the list of keys, while remaining expressive enough to cover common setups: singlesig, multisig, and MuSig2. This approach lets the user visually check the spending policy on the screen of their hardware wallet before signing a transaction.
