Proposal for an alias system that associates human-readable names with Bitcoin addresses through various resolution mechanisms. BIP-0015 aimed to simplify the user experience by replacing long alphanumeric addresses with memorable identifiers of the form `alias@domain.com`. Six mechanisms were proposed: FirstBits (using the blockchain as a directory), DNS TXT records, a dedicated service queried after a DNS resolution, resolution through an HTTPS request, IP Transactions complemented by a DNS lookup, and Namecoin (through a decentralized naming system). This BIP is a conceptual precursor of BIP-0353, which takes up the idea of human-readable addresses via DNS. It was never implemented and was closed.
