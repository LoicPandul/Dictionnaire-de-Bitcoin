Introduction and index document of the BOLT (Basis of Lightning Technology) specifications. It gives an overview of how the Lightning Network works, along with each BOLT. This introduction explains step by step how Lightning works: payment channels between two participants, conditional payments forwarded from hop to hop to link channels into a network, the broadcast of the network topology, and invoices. Each step points to the BOLT that details it. The document also includes a glossary of the terms used in the specifications.
