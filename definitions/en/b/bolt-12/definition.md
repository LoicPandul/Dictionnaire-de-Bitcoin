Specification that defines a Lightning payment negotiation protocol more flexible than BOLT-11. It introduces three concepts: offers (reusable payment templates that can be published through QR codes), invoice requests (requests for an invoice sent over the Lightning Network in response to an offer), and the invoices themselves. It also defines invoice errors, which report an explicit error in response to a request, and, since July 2026, payer proofs (prefix `lnp`), which prove that an invoice was paid by a given payer. A reverse flow exists: the merchant publishes an invoice request to send funds, for example for a vending machine or a refund.

Unlike single-use BOLT-11 invoices, BOLT-12 offers can be reused indefinitely and remain stateless on the merchant side. The format replaces the tagged fields of BOLT-11 with a more extensible TLV (Type-Length-Value) encoding, while the text form stays bech32-style. It supports selective disclosure of fields, allows cross-currency payments, and improves privacy through blinded paths that hide the recipient's identity.
