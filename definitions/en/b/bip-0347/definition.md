Proposal to re-enable the `OP_CAT` opcode in Tapscript only. `OP_CAT` concatenates the top two stack elements into one (that is, it joins them end to end), with a maximum result size of 520 bytes. This opcode was present in Bitcoin originally but was disabled by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2010 as a precaution, because of potential denial-of-service risks linked to the exponential growth of data on the stack. Re-enabling it in Tapscript would make it possible to build covenants, Merkle trees verifiable in Script, quantum-resistant Lamport signature schemes, and many other cryptographic primitives. BIP-0347, proposed by Ethan Heilman and Armin Sabouri, is one of the most debated soft fork proposals since the activation of Taproot, because of the variety of uses this simple opcode could open up. The BIP repository has listed it with status `Complete` since March 2026, not `Deployed`.
