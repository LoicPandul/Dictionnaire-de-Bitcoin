Reward offered for completing a specific task, often related to improving or securing a software project. Bounties are frequently used to encourage the discovery and fixing of software vulnerabilities, the development of new features, or the solving of complex problems. Participants, usually developers or researchers, receive a reward once their contributions have been verified and accepted. This incentive system speeds up development and strengthens the security of software by drawing in a wider community. Bounties are sometimes used by Bitcoin software projects.
