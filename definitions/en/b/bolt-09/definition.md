Specification that establishes the central registry of feature bits used in the Lightning Network to signal the features each node supports. Features are announced through flags in the `init`, `node_announcement`, and `channel_announcement` messages, as well as, depending on the feature, in BOLT-11 invoices and in the `allowed_features` field of blinded paths. Each feature uses a pair of bits: the even bit indicates mandatory support, and the odd bit indicates optional support.
