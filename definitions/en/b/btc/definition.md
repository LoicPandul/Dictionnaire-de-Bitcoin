Stock-market or monetary symbol (ticker) used to represent one unit of bitcoin on exchanges. It quickly identifies bitcoin among other assets and currencies. One unit of bitcoin (1 BTC) equals 100,000,000 satoshis (or “sats”).

*“BTC” is the historical symbol of bitcoin, but it does not comply with the ISO 4217 standard, which governs currency codes. Under that standard, “BT” is assigned to Bhutan, and currencies not backed by a state, such as bitcoin, should start with an “X”. This is why some organizations use the symbol “XBT” for bitcoin. Although “XBT” is not yet officially recognized by ISO 4217, it does meet its criteria.*
