Prolonged period during which the price of an asset, such as bitcoin, falls against a fiat currency, typically the US dollar. Halvings are scheduled events, every 210,000 blocks (about every four years), that cut the miners' reward in half. Some observers see in them the rhythm of a market cycle, with bear markets that would follow the bullish phases opened by each halving, but this link rests on a small number of episodes and no protocol rule imposes it.
