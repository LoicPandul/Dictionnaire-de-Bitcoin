Specification (status `Deployed` in the BIP repository since August 2026) describing Chain Code Delegation (CCD), a mechanism for multisig wallets in which a privileged participant (the "delegatee") holds back the BIP-0032 chain codes of one or more unprivileged participants (the "delegators") and supplies them with per-input scalar tweaks at signing time. Delegators hold only a plain key pair (no extended key), so they can neither derive other addresses of the wallet nor observe the balance or spending activity of the other cosigner combinations. This BIP is particularly useful in collaborative custody setups where the wallet owner wants to keep their balance private from their cosigner or custodian. BIP-0089 supports two signing modes: an unblinded mode, where the delegator sees the transaction it signs, and a blinded mode, where it sees neither the message nor the child public key used.
