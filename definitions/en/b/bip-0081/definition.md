Variant of BIP-0080 defining a hierarchical deterministic derivation path (BIP-0032) for voting pools that use colored coins with deterministic multisig wallets. BIP-0081 extended the scheme of BIP-0080 to tokenized assets by adding five extra derivation levels to encode the color definition (the hash160 of the definition, split into five 4-byte groups in little-endian) within the pool. Like BIP-0080, this proposal remained highly specialized and was never adopted by the Bitcoin ecosystem.
