Publicly traded company that places bitcoin at the center of its treasury strategy, accumulating bitcoin on its balance sheet instead of keeping its reserves in fiat currency or bonds. The goal is to protect against currency depreciation while offering shareholders indirect exposure to bitcoin through traditional shares.

MicroStrategy (now Strategy) popularized this model under Michael Saylor's leadership starting in August 2020. The company finances its bitcoin purchases by issuing convertible debt and shares, which creates leverage on the price of bitcoin. Several other companies have since adopted similar strategies, such as Metaplanet in Japan or Semler Scientific in the United States.

This model gives institutional investors who cannot hold bitcoin directly a way to gain access to it through a conventional stock vehicle, but it also exposes shareholders to the risks of financial leverage and of volatility in the underlying asset.
