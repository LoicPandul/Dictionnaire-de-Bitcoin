Mining protocol developed by Matt Corallo in 2018 to try to counter the growing centralization of mining in pools. It differs from Stratum, which was then the standard, by giving hashers more control over the selection of transactions to include in block templates. The main idea behind BetterHash is to give hashers back the ability to build the block template themselves, while keeping the benefits of mining pools such as reduced revenue variance.

In the Stratum protocol, mining pools control the creation of block templates, meaning they choose which transactions are included in blocks and also determine the chain to mine on. This centralization weakens Bitcoin, because it makes the transaction confirmation process vulnerable to censorship.

BetterHash lets hashers take back control of these operations, while leaving the pool to manage the distribution of rewards. It is therefore in a way one of the precursors of Stratum V2.
