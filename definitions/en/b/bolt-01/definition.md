Specification that defines the base protocol for messaging between Lightning nodes. It establishes the message format, their classification into five categories (setup, channel, commitment, routing, and custom messages), and the fundamental data types. BOLT-01 introduces the TLV (Type-Length-Value) format, an extensible encoding that lets implementations ignore unknown fields while maintaining compatibility. It also specifies the `init` message exchanged on connection between two peers to declare their features, the `error` and `warning` messages for error handling, and the `ping`/`pong` mechanism to keep connections alive.
