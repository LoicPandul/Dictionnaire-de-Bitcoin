Proposal for a dynamic block size limit in two phases. The first phase provided for scheduled increases: 2 MB in 2016-2017, 4 MB in 2018-2019, then 6 MB in 2020. The second phase introduced an automatic adjustment mechanism: every 4,032 blocks (about 4 weeks), if more than 75% of blocks were at least 60% full, the maximum size increased by 10%. This mechanism allowed only increases, never decreases. BIP-0107 has the status `Closed` and was never deployed on Bitcoin.
