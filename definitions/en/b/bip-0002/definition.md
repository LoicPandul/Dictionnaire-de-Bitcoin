Document written by Luke Dashjr in 2016 that revises the Bitcoin Improvement Proposal (BIP) process defined by BIP-0001. BIP-0002 clarifies the possible statuses of proposals (`Draft`, `Proposed`, `Final`, `Deferred`, `Withdrawn`, `Rejected`, `Replaced`, `Obsolete`, `Active`) and sets objective criteria for moving from one status to another. In particular, it introduces concrete rules for a BIP to reach `Final` status: for example, a soft fork requires a clear majority of miners, while an application-layer BIP must be implemented by at least two independent software projects. BIP-0002 also adds a public comment system, a classification by layer taken from BIP-0123, and licensing requirements for new proposals. This process has since been replaced by that of BIP-0003. Its status is `Closed`.
