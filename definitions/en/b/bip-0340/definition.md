Proposal that defines the Schnorr signature scheme for Bitcoin's secp256k1 curve, activated with SegWit v1 (Taproot) in November 2021. Compared with ECDSA, Schnorr signatures offer several advantages: they are mathematically simpler, have a formal security proof, and natively enable key aggregation (MuSig2), batch verification, and more compact multisignatures. BIP-0340 uses 64-byte signatures (with no DER encoding) and 32-byte x-only public keys, which reduces the size of on-chain data.
