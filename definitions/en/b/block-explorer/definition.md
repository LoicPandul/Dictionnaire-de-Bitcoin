Online or local tool that turns the raw data of the Bitcoin blockchain into a structured format that humans can easily read. An explorer usually includes a search engine to easily locate a specific block, transaction, or address.

An online explorer indexes the data of a full node that it runs itself, and it sees the addresses and transactions that its visitors look up. Some open-source explorers, such as mempool.space or Esplora, can also be self-hosted on one's own node, which makes it possible to browse the blockchain without exposing queries to a third party.
