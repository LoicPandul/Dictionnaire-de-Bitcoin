Proposal suggesting a lexicographic sort of the inputs and outputs of a Bitcoin transaction to reduce the possibilities of identification by fingerprinting. Inputs are sorted by `txid` and then by output index (`vout`), and outputs by amount and then by `scriptPubKey`. The goal is to improve privacy by making transactions produced by different software more uniform, which makes it harder to identify the wallet used. However, this BIP is now considered insufficient for privacy, because a deterministic sort can itself become an identifiable fingerprint.
