Open-source library that implements the Bitcoin protocol in Java, created by Mike Hearn in 2011. It is designed to run in SPV (Simplified Payment Verification) mode: instead of downloading the entire blockchain, it fetches only block headers and uses Bloom filters to identify relevant transactions. This lightweight operation makes it suitable for mobile devices and resource-constrained environments. Bitcoinj has served as the basis for many projects, including the Bitcoin Wallet Android app, Bisq, and RSK.
