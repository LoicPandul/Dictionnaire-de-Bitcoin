Acronym for *Better Bitcoin QR*. Protocol that encodes files too large for a single QR code into a series of successive QR codes (animated QR). The target data types are PSBTs (BIP-0174) and signed transactions, but the format also supports CBOR, JSON, and text data.

The protocol is adopted by several software products and signing devices, notably COLDCARD Q, Sparrow Wallet, and Nunchuk. The specification is published by Coinkite as an open standard.
