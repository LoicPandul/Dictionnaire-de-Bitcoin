Open-source decentralized exchange (DEX) software for buying and selling bitcoin peer-to-peer, with no centralized intermediary and no identity verification (KYC). Bisq runs as a desktop application that connects to a P2P network over Tor. Each trade is secured by a bitcoin security deposit (2-of-2 multisig) and an arbitration process in case of dispute. Trades are made against fiat currencies (bank transfer, Revolut, etc.) or other cryptocurrencies. Bisq never holds users' funds and collects no personal data. The software is funded by an integrated DAO (decentralized autonomous organization) that uses the BSQ token for its governance.
