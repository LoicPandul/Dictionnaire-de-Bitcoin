Proposal known as “Erlay” that aims to optimize the propagation of unconfirmed transactions across the Bitcoin network. Without Erlay, transaction identifiers are announced between every pair of connected peers, which causes redundancy and excessive bandwidth use. BIP-0330 specifies an extension of the P2P protocol that limits this announcement to a small subset of connections, then uses a reconciliation mechanism to efficiently share the missing transactions. According to the research paper behind Erlay (2019), this approach reduces the bandwidth consumed by transaction announcements by about 40%, with the connectivity of the time. It also prevents bandwidth consumption from growing linearly with the number of peers connected to the node.
