Mobile Bitcoin wallet, formerly known as “Bitcoin Beach Wallet”. Developed by Galoy, it was originally created for the Bitcoin Beach community in El Zonte, El Salvador. It manages BTC and USD balances and sends and receives Lightning and on-chain payments. The legacy account is custodial, and its USD balance relies on Stablesats. Since June 2026, Blink also offers non-custodial accounts built on the Spark protocol.
