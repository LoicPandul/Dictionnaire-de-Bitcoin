Asset or financial instrument whose ownership is determined solely by physical possession, without a centralized registry, an intermediary, or a named entry. Whoever holds the asset is its legitimate owner. Banknotes, gold coins, and bearer bonds are examples of bearer assets.

This type of asset does not depend on any third party to validate or authorize a transfer of ownership. It gives its holder direct sovereignty, but also responsibility: loss or theft means the permanent loss of the asset.

Bitcoin held in self-custody is a digital bearer asset. Possession of the private keys that secure the bitcoin gives exclusive control over the funds, with no bank, institution, or centralized registry involved.
