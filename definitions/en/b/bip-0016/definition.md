BIP that introduced the concept of Pay-to-Script-Hash (P2SH). First proposed in January 2012 and activated in April 2012, BIP-0016 aimed to simplify the use of transactions that require complex scripts, such as multisignature transactions, by letting users pay to a hash of the script required to spend those bitcoin rather than to the script itself. This innovation reduced the amount of data needed in the initial transaction, shifting the burden of providing the full script to the party spending the bitcoin. It also made it possible to reveal the script only when the bitcoin committed to it are spent, rather than as soon as they are received. BIP-0016 has historical significance because it marks one of the first major changes to the Bitcoin protocol after Nakamoto's withdrawal in 2011. This BIP was at the center of very tense debates that even led Gavin Andresen, Satoshi's successor as lead maintainer, to give himself a period of withdrawal. Many other proposals existed, and some nearly got activated in place of BIP-0016.
