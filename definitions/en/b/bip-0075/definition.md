Extension of the BIP-0070 payment protocol that introduces two major improvements. First, it lets the sender of a payment request voluntarily sign that request and provide a certificate, so that the recipient can know the identity of the other party. Second, it encrypts the returned payment request to prevent interception and viewing by third parties. This strengthens the security and privacy of transactions by ensuring that the payment details are visible only to the participants. BIP-0075 also offers new features that improve the user experience. Because this improvement builds on BIP-0070, it was never widely adopted.
