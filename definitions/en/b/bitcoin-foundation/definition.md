Nonprofit organization founded in September 2012. Its founding members are Gavin Andresen, Charlie Shrem, Mark Karpelès, Peter Vessenes, Roger Ver, and Patrick Murck. It aimed to promote the development of Bitcoin, fund protocol developers, and represent the community before institutions.

In its early years, the foundation funded the work of developers such as Gavin Andresen and contributed to the standardization of the protocol. It was quickly confronted with controversy, however. Several of its founding members faced legal proceedings: Charlie Shrem was arrested in 2014 for money laundering linked to the Silk Road marketplace, and Mark Karpelès was indicted in Japan after the collapse of Mt. Gox.

The foundation lost influence over the years, criticized for its governance and its lack of financial transparency. Part of the Bitcoin community reproached it for trying to centralize the representation of a project designed to be decentralized.
