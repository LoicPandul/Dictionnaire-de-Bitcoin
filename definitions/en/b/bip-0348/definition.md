Proposal to add the `OP_CHECKSIGFROMSTACK` opcode to Tapscript, replacing `OP_SUCCESS204`. Unlike `OP_CHECKSIG`, which verifies a signature over the hash of the current transaction, this opcode verifies a Schnorr signature (BIP-0340) over an arbitrary message provided on the stack. It consumes three elements: a 32-byte public key, a message, and a signature. The BIP deliberately defines no VERIFY variant, for simplicity. Combined with `OP_TXHASH` (BIP-0346), it enables a fully generalized signature hash construction, paving the way for transaction introspection, delegation of spending rights, and Lightning Symmetry channels (LN-Symmetry / Eltoo). Proposed by Brandon Black and Jeremy Rubin, its status is `Draft`.
