bLIP that allows the second-to-last hop of a Lightning route to forward an HTLC for an amount lower than the one encoded in the onion packet. The relaying node declares the additional fees it deducted through a new TLV field called `extra_fee`. This mechanism is mainly designed for Lightning service providers (LSPs) that open just-in-time channels for new users. By deducting additional fees from the first payment received, the LSP can offset the on-chain cost of opening the channel. The recipient explicitly checks the amount of fees deducted, which prevents any abuse by the intermediate node.
