Specification BIP that introduces the derivation method used to generate Nested SegWit addresses in an HD wallet. The proposed derivation path follows the standards of BIP-0043 and BIP-0044, with the index `49'` (hardened derivation) at the purpose depth. For example, the first address of a P2SH-P2WPKH account would come from the path `m/49'/0'/0'/0/0`. Nested SegWit scripts were invented at the launch of SegWit to ease its adoption. They make it possible to use this new standard even on wallets that are not yet natively compatible with SegWit.
