Definition of a standard proof-of-reserves format based on intentionally invalid Bitcoin transactions. With `Complete` status in the BIP repository since May 2026, BIP-0127 lets custodians prove cryptographically that they control certain UTXOs without having to move them. The proof transaction includes a first input, called the "commitment input," whose `txid` is replaced by the SHA-256 hash of a proof message. This fictitious input is what makes the transaction invalid and impossible to confirm on the blockchain. The other inputs are signed normally and correspond to the funds held. The transaction has a single output whose amount equals the sum of all inputs, with no mining fee. BIP-0127 builds on the PSBT format of BIP-0174 to make it easier to construct proofs with existing wallet infrastructure, and also describes a PSBT extension to improve the user experience.
