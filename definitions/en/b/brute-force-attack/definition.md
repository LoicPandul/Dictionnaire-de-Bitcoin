Cryptanalysis method for finding a password or key that consists of trying, by trial and error, every possible combination of keys or passwords until finding the one that grants access to a protected privilege or piece of information. This technique relies on intensive computation and can take an extremely long time, especially against large keys. To counter this type of attack, longer passwords and keys must be used in order to multiply the number of operations required of the attacker. In theory, the complexity of such an attack is exponential in the length of the target.
