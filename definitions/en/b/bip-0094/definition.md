Defines Testnet 4, a new test network meant to replace Testnet 3 in 2024. The latter, which had been in service for 13 years, had become vulnerable to various attacks and had been the scene of abusive use through fraudulent altcoin airdrops, disrupting the normally free distribution of TBTC. To fix these flaws, Testnet 4 introduces 3 major adjustments to the consensus rules: a change to the 20-minute exception, a fix to the difficulty adjustment algorithm (the block storm fix), and protection against the "time warp" attack.
