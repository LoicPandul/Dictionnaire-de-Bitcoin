Proposal of several rules to solve Bitcoin transaction malleability, including strict DER encoding of signatures, canonical push data, and other constraints on the format of scripts. BIP-0062 is an important piece of preparatory work that influenced the design of SegWit. It was withdrawn (marked `Withdrawn` in 2015, now `Closed` in the BIP repository), because its rules were judged insufficient to cover all the use cases that require malleability to be solved (such as payment channels) and too complex to implement as consensus rules. The malleability problem was ultimately solved more completely by SegWit (BIP-0141), which separates the witness data from the computation of the TXID.
