Bitcoin is the name of the peer-to-peer electronic cash system created by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2009. Using the term Bitcoin with a capital "B" can refer to three different things:
* The Bitcoin system;
* The Bitcoin protocol;
* The Bitcoin network.

The term bitcoin with a lowercase "b" is generally reserved for the monetary unit exchanged on this system.
