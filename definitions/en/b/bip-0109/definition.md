Proposal presented by Gavin Andresen in January 2016 as part of the Blocksize War. It aimed to raise the maximum block size from 1 MB to 2 MB through a hard fork, while adding certain limits on the number of sigops and sighashes (unlike BIP-0102). Activating BIP-0109 required the support of 75% of the hash power over a window of 1,000 blocks, followed by a grace period of 28 days. This proposal was an integral part of the Bitcoin Classic fork, which sought to offer a solution for scaling Bitcoin. The proposal was never adopted.
