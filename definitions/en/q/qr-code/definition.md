Type of two-dimensional barcode that can be scanned quickly by mobile devices. The QR code stores information as black and white pixels arranged in a square on a white background. An important feature of QR codes is that they embed Reed-Solomon error correction codes that make it possible to restore the data even if the QR code is partially obscured.

The QR code is widely used in Bitcoin to facilitate the transfer of information between two devices. It is mainly used to share receiving addresses, encoded in URI format according to BIP-0021. But its use extends also to extended keys, PSBTs, Lightning invoices, and more.

*QR code stands for "Quick Response code."*
