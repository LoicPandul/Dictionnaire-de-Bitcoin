Open-source firmware that turns devices built on the Kendryte K210 microcontroller, such as the Maix Amigo or the M5StickV, into Bitcoin transaction signing devices. Krux can generate and manage private keys, sign transactions offline, and interact with coordinator wallets (Sparrow, Specter...) through QR codes or microSD cards. The project targets users who want to build a low-cost hardware wallet from generic off-the-shelf hardware while keeping full control over the source code.
