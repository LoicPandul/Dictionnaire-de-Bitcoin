Coin selection algorithm of the Bitcoin Core wallet. The Knapsack Solver tries to solve the coin selection problem by iteratively and randomly picking UTXOs and adding them up by subsets, with the goal of minimizing fees and transaction size. Branch-and-Bound joined it in Bitcoin Core 0.17, and the wallet now also runs Single Random Draw and, at a high fee rate, CoinGrinder (since Bitcoin Core 27.0). It keeps the result that produces the least waste.
