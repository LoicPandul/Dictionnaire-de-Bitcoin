Head of the Mt. Gox exchange from 2011 until its bankruptcy in 2014, known online under the pseudonym MagicalTux.

On March 6, 2011, Jed McCaleb announced on the Bitcointalk forum that he was passing the reins to MagicalTux. MagicalTux replied in the same thread that Mt. Gox would be run by his own company, which would hire staff. According to the press, McCaleb sold most of the business to Karpelès in 2011.

On February 28, 2014, Mt. Gox asked the Tokyo court to open civil rehabilitation proceedings, which the press described as bankruptcy protection. The court rejected the rehabilitation request on April 16, 2014, then opened bankruptcy proceedings on April 24, 2014.

Karpelès was subsequently prosecuted in Japan. On March 15, 2019, the Tokyo District Court sentenced him to 2 years and 6 months in prison, suspended for 4 years, for manipulating electronic data, and acquitted him of the embezzlement charges. Prosecutors had sought 10 years.
