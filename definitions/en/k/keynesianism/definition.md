School of economic thought based on the work of the British economist John Maynard Keynes. Keynesianism advocates active state intervention in the economy to stabilize economic cycles. Keynesian policies rest on the ability of central banks to adjust the money supply and interest rates, and on the ability of governments to borrow and spend. This theoretical framework largely dominates contemporary economic policy.

In the Bitcoin community, Keynesianism is often criticized. Bitcoiners, generally close to the Austrian school, blame it for encouraging monetary expansion, distorting price signals through interest rate manipulation, favoring malinvestment, and generating growing public debt. Bitcoin, with its fixed supply and the impossibility of manipulating it monetarily, is fundamentally incompatible with Keynesian monetary policy tools.
