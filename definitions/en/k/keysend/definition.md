Payment method on the Lightning Network that sends funds directly to a node's public key, without the recipient having to generate an invoice beforehand. Unlike the classic BOLT-11 flow, where the recipient chooses the preimage and passes it along through the invoice, here the sender generates the preimage and includes it in the payment's encrypted onion packet. Only the recipient node can decrypt the packet and retrieve the preimage to settle the HTLC.

The recipient node must explicitly enable keysend reception (`accept-keysend=true` in the LND configuration). Arbitrary data can be attached to the payment through TLV records, which makes keysend useful for donations, streaming payments (Podcasting 2.0), or messaging between nodes. The main limitation is the absence of proof of payment: since the sender chooses the preimage itself, it cannot prove after the fact that it made the payment.
